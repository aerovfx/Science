## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Although/Though + clause, clause.` | Nối hai ý tương phản trong cùng câu. | Although the plot is simple, the film is moving. / The film is funny though its ending is sad. |
| `Sentence. However, sentence.` | Mở đầu câu thứ hai để nêu ý tương phản; có dấu phẩy sau however. | The film is long. However, it never feels boring. / The actors are young. However, they perform confidently. |

## 3. Pronunciation

- focus: Sound /ɪə/ in idea and /eə/ in character-related words; examples: ['idea, theatre; pair, share, prepare']; tips_vi: Giữ luồng hơi liên tục khi chuyển giữa hai vị trí nguyên âm.

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
