## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `It is + distance + from A to B.` | Nêu khoảng cách giữa hai địa điểm. | It is two kilometres from my home to school. / How far is it from here to the station? |
| `S + should/shouldn't + base verb.` | Đưa ra lời khuyên về an toàn giao thông. | You should use the crossing. / Cyclists shouldn't ride on the pavement. |

## 3. Pronunciation

- focus: Sound /aɪ/ in ride and /eɪ/ in safe; examples: ['bike, ride, sign; lane, train, safe']; tips_vi: Kéo nhẹ nguyên âm đôi và không tách thành hai âm rời.

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
