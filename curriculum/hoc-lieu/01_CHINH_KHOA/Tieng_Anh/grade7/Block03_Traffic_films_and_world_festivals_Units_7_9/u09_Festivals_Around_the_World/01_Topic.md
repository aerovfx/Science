# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 9: FESTIVALS AROUND THE WORLD
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 7 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Describe festival times, activities and meanings
- Ask and answer yes-no questions about celebrations
- Compare responsible ways to join festivals
- Present an international festival respectfully

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Do/Does + subject + base verb ...?` | Đặt câu hỏi Có/Không ở hiện tại đơn. | Do people wear costumes? / Does the parade start at noon? |
| `Is/Are + subject + complement ...?` | Hỏi về trạng thái, đặc điểm hoặc sự tồn tại. | Is the festival free? / Are fireworks part of the celebration? |
| `Did + subject + base verb ...?` | Hỏi Có/Không về hoạt động đã xảy ra. | Did you join the parade? / Did they enjoy the music? |

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 9: Festivals Around the World** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Mai: Shall we talk about interviewing a visitor at a lantern festival?  
Ben: Yes. What information do we need first?  
Mai: We need a clear purpose and two reliable facts.  
Ben: I found one useful idea, but I want to check it.  
Mai: Good. We should also consider safety and other people's needs.  
Ben: Agreed. What will we present to the class?  
Mai: Let's make a short dialogue and a simple poster.  
Ben: I'll draft the poster. Can you check the language?  
Mai: Of course. Then we can practise together.  
Ben: Great. Our presentation will be ready tomorrow.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Welcome to the student information desk. The World Festival begins at ten o'clock on Saturday. Does the opening parade start at the east gate? Yes, it does, and it finishes near the music stage. Are costumes required? No, visitors may wear ordinary clothes, but performers will wear traditional dress. Do food stalls accept cash? Some do, though using festival tokens is faster. You can buy tokens beside the main entrance. Is the dance workshop suitable for beginners? Yes, the teachers will demonstrate every step slowly. Did you register for the storytelling session online? If not, there are still twenty places available. Finally, are visitors allowed to photograph every ceremony? No. Please look for the camera sign and ask participants when you are uncertain. Bring a reusable bottle because free water stations are located around the park.

**Tasks:**

1. Identify the setting and main purpose.
2. Write two key factual details.
3. Note one instruction or recommendation.
4. Identify one reason given by the speaker.
5. Summarise the message in 20–30 words.

**Answer key:** A spoken information segment about festivals around the world.; Accept any two accurate details from the script.; Accept one explicit instruction or recommendation from the script.; Accept a correct cause-and-effect detail from the script.; Answers vary; the summary must include the topic and most important action or conclusion.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Every June, the small town of Solvik holds a Sunlight Festival. People wear yellow clothes, share music, and decorate bicycles with flowers. In the past, the event created a great deal of rubbish because visitors used plastic cups and left decorations in the park. Did the town cancel its popular celebration? No, it redesigned it. Today, food stalls lend reusable plates and cups. Are visitors required to return them? Yes, they pay a small deposit and receive it back later. Local artists make decorations from cloth, wood, and dried plants. The evening parade still looks bright, but electric lights replace noisy fireworks. Does everyone like the changes? Most residents do because streets are cleaner and pets are calmer. Some visitors miss the old firework display; however, they enjoy a new light show projected onto the town hall. Student volunteers also explain the festival's history in several languages. Solvik proves that a community can protect a joyful tradition while changing habits that harm the environment.

**Comprehension:**

1. What is the main purpose of the text about Festivals Around the World?
2. Name two details that support the main idea.
3. What problem or challenge is mentioned?
4. How do people respond to that challenge?
5. What lesson can a Grade 7 learner apply?

**Answers:** It explains a responsible project or informed perspective connected with the unit topic.; Accept any two accurate details quoted or paraphrased from the passage.; Accept the specific difficulty identified in the passage.; They plan, cooperate, measure results, redesign an action, or learn from evidence.; Answers vary but must connect a practical action to evidence in the text.

**Writing:**

1. Write an 100-word introduction to an international festival.
2. Prepare six yes-no interview questions and plausible answers.
3. Design a visitor guide that includes respectful behaviour.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Vocabulary sort: classify the festivals around the world words and justify each group.
- Information gap: partners hold different facts and must ask complete questions.
- Grammar detective: identify target forms in original sentences and explain their use.
- Role-play: solve a realistic communication problem politely in pairs.
- Exit ticket: write one accurate sentence, one question, and one personal reflection.

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Using the target form without considering its communicative meaning.; correction: Ask whether the sentence expresses the intended time, contrast, question, ownership, advice, or reference.
- issue: Translating Vietnamese word order directly into English.; correction: Use the model pattern, then check subject, auxiliary, main verb, and punctuation.
- issue: Memorising vocabulary without context.; correction: Record collocations and write a personal example for every new word.
- issue: Treating one example as true for every culture or situation.; correction: Use careful language, verify facts, and recognise varied experiences.
