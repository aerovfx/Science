# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Review 4: Energy, Future Travel and English-speaking Countries

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

We are visiting the museum on Monday. / Students practise Present continuous for arrangements in a short exchange.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Write an 80–100 word response about energy sources, future travel and English-speaking countries.
2. Create a practical notice or plan with purpose, details and one safety note.
3. Revise a first draft using two grammar patterns and six review words.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Review 4: Energy, Future Travel and English-speaking Countries**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
