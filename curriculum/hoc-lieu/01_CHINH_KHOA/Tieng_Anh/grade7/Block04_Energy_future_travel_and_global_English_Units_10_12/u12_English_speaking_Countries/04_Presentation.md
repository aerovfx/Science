# NÓI VÀ THUYẾT TRÌNH / SPEAKING & PRESENTATION
## Unit 12: English-speaking Countries

## Task

Prepare a 2–3 minute pair/group presentation for classmates.

## Suggested structure

1. Greeting and topic.
2. Show 4–6 picture/word cards.
3. Perform a target dialogue.
4. Share one personal example.
5. Ask the class one question.
6. Close and thank the audience.

## Useful language

- a + singular consonant-sound noun; an + singular vowel-sound noun
- the + specific/unique noun
- zero article + plural/uncountable general noun

## Activities

- Vocabulary sort: classify the english-speaking countries words and justify each group.
- Information gap: partners hold different facts and must ask complete questions.
- Grammar detective: identify target forms in original sentences and explain their use.
- Role-play: solve a realistic communication problem politely in pairs.
- Exit ticket: write one accurate sentence, one question, and one personal reflection.

## Speaking rubric

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Message | Hard to understand | Some clear ideas | Clear and relevant | Clear with added detail |
| Language | Many missing words | Uses a frame | Accurate target pattern | Flexible use |
| Pronunciation | Often unclear | Understandable with repetition | Mostly clear | Clear rhythm and confidence |
| Interaction | No turn-taking | Needs prompts | Takes turns | Responds and follows up |
