## Seven-period unit rhythm

1. Getting Started: context and communicative purpose.
2. A Closer Look 1: vocabulary and pronunciation.
3. A Closer Look 2: grammar in context.
4. Communication: everyday English and interaction.
5. Skills 1: reading and speaking.
6. Skills 2: listening and writing.
7. Looking Back & Project: consolidation, product and assessment.
