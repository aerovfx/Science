# KHUNG ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

## Principles

- Assess communicative success, accuracy, comprehension, interaction and progress.
- Use observation, portfolios, short performances, listening/reading evidence and writing samples.
- Provide child-friendly feedback: one success, one next step.

| Skill | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Recognises isolated words | Understands with repetition | Gets gist and details | Explains evidence |
| Speaking | Uses prompted words | Gives patterned responses | Completes clear exchanges | Adds details/questions |
| Reading | Needs decoding support | Finds some information | Understands short texts | Makes simple inference |
| Writing | Copies words | Completes frames | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Rarely participates | Responds with support | Takes turns politely | Sustains and supports exchange |

## Evidence portfolio

- One speaking sample, one listening check, one reading response and one writing product per block.
- Review products and self-assessment after Units 3, 6, 9 and 12.
