# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 6: A VISIT TO A SCHOOL
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 7 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Describe school places and facilities
- Give directions around a campus
- Discuss schedules and school history
- Plan and report a respectful visit

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `at + clock time; on + day/date; in + month/year/period` | Giới từ chỉ thời gian. | The tour begins at 8:30. / We visit on Monday. / The school opened in 1995. |
| `in/on/at + place` | Chỉ vị trí theo không gian hoặc điểm cụ thể. | Books are in the library. / Meet at the gate. / The notice is on the wall. |
| `between, opposite, next to, behind, near` | Mô tả quan hệ vị trí và chỉ đường. | The lab is next to Room 7. / The garden is behind the hall. |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /tʃ/ and /dʒ/; examples: ['chair, teacher', 'gym, large']; tips_vi: ['Cả hai bắt đầu bằng chặn hơi; /dʒ/ có rung thanh.', 'Không thêm nguyên âm sau âm cuối.']

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 6: A Visit to a School** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Guide: Welcome to River School.  
Lan: Where does the tour begin?  
Guide: At the history room beside the entrance.  
Lan: Is the science lab near it?  
Guide: No, it is on the second floor.  
Lan: When can we see the library?  
Guide: At ten fifteen, after the art room.  
Lan: May we take photographs?  
Guide: Yes, of displays, but ask before photographing people.  
Lan: Understood. Thank you.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Good morning, Grade 7 visitors. Please arrive at North Gate at eight forty-five on Wednesday. Do not use West Gate because construction work is taking place there. At nine, guides will lead groups to the assembly hall, which is opposite the music room. The welcome ends at nine twenty. Group One then visits the library, while Group Two starts in the technology lab; groups change places at ten. Keep food and drinks outside both rooms. At eleven, meet in the garden behind the canteen for the environmental activity. In heavy rain, this activity moves to Room 14. Wear your visitor badge during the whole tour and stay with your group. If you need step-free access or other support, tell your teacher before Tuesday so the route can be prepared.

**Tasks:**

1. Which gate and time?
2. Why not West Gate?
3. Where is the hall?
4. When do groups swap?
5. How can access support be arranged?

**Answer key:** North Gate at 8:45 Wednesday.; Construction work.; Opposite the music room.; At 10:00.; Tell the teacher before Tuesday.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Students from Hill School visited Riverside School on 12 October. They arrived at 8:15 and met their partners at the main gate. Riverside was founded in 1978, but its newest building opened in 2022. During the morning tour, visitors saw a history room in the old hall and a robotics laboratory in the new block. The contrast helped them discuss how education changes while school memories remain important. At ten o'clock, mixed teams solved a campus map challenge. They had to find clues near the library, between the two gardens, and opposite the canteen. After lunch, students shared short presentations about daily life at each school. Hill School had a larger playground, while Riverside offered more indoor clubs. The visit ended with feedback cards. Students praised the friendly guides but suggested clearer signs for wheelchair-accessible routes. Teachers agreed to improve the next visit. The day showed that a school exchange is more than seeing buildings; it is a chance to compare experiences, practise hospitality, and make learning spaces more inclusive.

**Comprehension:**

1. When did students visit?
2. What opened in 2022?
3. What happened at ten?
4. What did Hill School have?
5. What improvement was suggested?

**Answers:** On 12 October.; Riverside's newest building.; A campus map challenge.; A larger playground.; Clearer signs for wheelchair-accessible routes.

**Writing:**

1. Write directions from the gate to three facilities.
2. Create a timed visit itinerary using at/on/in.
3. Write a 130-word visit report with one accessibility recommendation.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Campus map information gap
- Preposition treasure hunt
- Visitor-guide role-play
- Compare school facilities
- Design an accessible tour

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |
