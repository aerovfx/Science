# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 6: A Visit to a School

### Dialogue 1

Guide: Welcome to River School.  
Lan: Where does the tour begin?  
Guide: At the history room beside the entrance.  
Lan: Is the science lab near it?  
Guide: No, it is on the second floor.  
Lan: When can we see the library?  
Guide: At ten fifteen, after the art room.  
Lan: May we take photographs?  
Guide: Yes, of displays, but ask before photographing people.  
Lan: Understood. Thank you.

### Dialogue 2

Ben: Excuse me, how do I get to the gym?  
Student: Walk past the library and turn left.  
Ben: Is it opposite the canteen?  
Student: Almost. It is between the canteen and playground.  
Ben: Does basketball start at two?  
Student: It starts at two thirty today.  
Ben: Thanks. Where should visitors wait?  
Student: At the blue door near the sports office.  
Ben: That is very helpful.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
