# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 6: A Visit to a School

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

The tour begins at 8:30. / We visit on Monday. / The school opened in 1995.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Write directions from the gate to three facilities.
2. Create a timed visit itinerary using at/on/in.
3. Write a 130-word visit report with one accessibility recommendation.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 6: A Visit to a School**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
