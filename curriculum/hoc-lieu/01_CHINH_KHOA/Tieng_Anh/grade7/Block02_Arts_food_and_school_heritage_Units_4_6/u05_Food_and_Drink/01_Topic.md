## UNIT 5: FOOD AND DRINK
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Order food politely
- Discuss quantities and ingredients
- Describe a simple recipe
- Plan a balanced affordable meal

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `some + plural/uncountable noun; any in negatives/questions` | Nói về lượng không xác định. | We need some eggs. / Is there any milk? / There isn't any sugar. |
| `How many + plural count noun?` | Hỏi số lượng danh từ đếm được. | How many tomatoes do we need? |
| `How much + uncountable noun?` | Hỏi lượng hoặc giá. | How much rice is there? / How much is the soup? |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /ɒ/ and /ɔː/; examples: ['bottle, hot', 'sauce, fork']; tips_vi: ['/ɒ/ ngắn; /ɔː/ dài hơn và môi tròn.', 'Nghe và bắt chước theo giọng mẫu vì biến thể vùng miền có thể khác.']

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 5: Food and Drink** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Server: Good evening. Are you ready to order?  
Minh: Yes. I'd like vegetable noodles, please.  
Server: Would you like any soup?  
Minh: No, thank you. How much are the noodles?  
Server: They are fifty thousand dong.  
Minh: Does the dish contain peanuts?  
Server: No, but it is prepared in a shared kitchen.  
Minh: Thanks for telling me. I'll have water too.  
Server: Certainly.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> For tomorrow's cooking club, we are making tomato and bean soup for eight people. Please check the list carefully. We already have some onions, salt, and cooking oil in the cupboard. We need six tomatoes, two cans of beans, and one litre of vegetable stock. There isn't any bread, so buy one loaf. We also need a small bunch of herbs, but do not buy cream because one member cannot have milk products. Bring the receipt and keep cold ingredients cool. At the club, wash your hands, tie back long hair, and use knives only at your assigned station with teacher supervision. Taste with a clean spoon each time. The soup should simmer for twenty minutes; it does not need to boil strongly.

**Tasks:**

1. How many servings?
2. Which items are already available?
3. How much stock is needed?
4. Why is cream excluded?
5. Give two kitchen safety rules.

**Answer key:** Eight.; Onions, salt, and oil.; One litre.; A member cannot have milk products.; Any two stated rules.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Class 7B planned a picnic for thirty students. At first, their shopping list included many small juice boxes, plastic spoons, and individual snack packets. Then the eco-club challenged them to reduce waste. The class counted what they really needed. They prepared two large containers of lemon water, and everyone brought a reusable cup. Families made vegetable rolls, fruit salad, and rice balls. Ingredient cards showed whether dishes contained eggs, milk, nuts, or seafood. This helped students with allergies and different diets choose safely. The class bought some bananas but did not buy any delicate fruit because it might be damaged on the journey. They also carried one clean container for leftovers. After lunch, only a handful of fruit peels remained, and these went into compost. The meal was not expensive, and there was enough food without too much waste. Their teacher said good planning meant asking not only “How much?” and “How many?” but also “Who can eat this safely?” and “What happens to the packaging?”

**Comprehension:**

1. How many students attended?
2. What replaced juice boxes?
3. Why were ingredients labelled?
4. Why did they avoid delicate fruit?
5. What did good planning include?

**Answers:** Thirty.; Large lemon-water containers and reusable cups.; To support allergy and diet safety.; It might be damaged.; Quantity, safety, inclusion, and waste.

**Writing:**

1. Write a shopping list with quantities.
2. Write a six-step safe recipe.
3. Design a menu description including diet and allergy information.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Countable/uncountable market
- Restaurant role-play
- Recipe sequencing
- Budget meal challenge
- Food-waste audit

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: How many rice?; correction: How much rice?
- issue: Any is never used in positive requests.; correction: Some is common in offers/requests; usage depends on meaning.
- issue: Food preferences and allergies are the same.; correction: Allergies can be medically serious and require clear information.
- issue: More food is always better hospitality.; correction: Plan enough portions while limiting waste.
