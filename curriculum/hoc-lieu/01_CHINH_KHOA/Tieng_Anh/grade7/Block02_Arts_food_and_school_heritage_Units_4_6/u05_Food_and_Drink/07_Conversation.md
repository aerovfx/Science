# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 5: Food and Drink

### Dialogue 1

Server: Good evening. Are you ready to order?  
Minh: Yes. I'd like vegetable noodles, please.  
Server: Would you like any soup?  
Minh: No, thank you. How much are the noodles?  
Server: They are fifty thousand dong.  
Minh: Does the dish contain peanuts?  
Server: No, but it is prepared in a shared kitchen.  
Minh: Thanks for telling me. I'll have water too.  
Server: Certainly.

### Dialogue 2

Nga: How many sandwiches should we make?  
Sam: Twelve, but two guests are vegetarian.  
Nga: We have some cheese and tomatoes.  
Sam: Is there any cucumber?  
Nga: Only one. Let's buy two more.  
Sam: How much bread do we need?  
Nga: Two loaves should be enough.  
Sam: I'll label the ingredients for allergies.  
Nga: Good. Everyone should know what is inside.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
- Listen again and choose one pronunciation point to improve.
