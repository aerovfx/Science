## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `as + adjective + as / not as ... as` | So sánh bằng hoặc không bằng. | The flute is as expressive as the violin. / This hall is not as large as that one. |
| `be different from` | Nêu sự khác biệt. | Watercolour is different from oil painting. |
| `be like + noun/pronoun` | Nêu sự tương đồng hoặc đặc điểm giống. | The melody is like a gentle river. / Her voice is like her mother's. |

## 3. Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /ʃ/ and /ʒ/; examples: ['show, musician', 'vision, usual']; tips_vi: ['/ʃ/ không rung thanh; /ʒ/ có rung thanh.', 'Giữ môi hơi tròn và luồng hơi liên tục.']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
