# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 4: Music and Arts

### Dialogue 1

Nhi: Which picture do you prefer?  
Tom: The river landscape.  
Nhi: Why?  
Tom: Its colours are as calm as the evening sky.  
Nhi: I prefer the portrait.  
Tom: Is it like the artist's other work?  
Nhi: No, it is different from her bright paintings.  
Tom: The expression is powerful.  
Nhi: Yes. It makes me curious about the person.

### Dialogue 2

Leo: We need one act for the arts festival.  
Ha: A drum performance could be exciting.  
Leo: Is it as suitable as a choir for our small hall?  
Ha: Drums are louder, but we can control the volume.  
Leo: Can beginners join?  
Ha: Yes. We can create simple rhythms.  
Leo: Let's combine drums with a projected animation.  
Ha: Great. Music and visual art can tell one story.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
- Listen again and choose one pronunciation point to improve.
