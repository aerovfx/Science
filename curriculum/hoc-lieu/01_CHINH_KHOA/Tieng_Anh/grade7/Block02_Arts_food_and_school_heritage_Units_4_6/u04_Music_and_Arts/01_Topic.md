## UNIT 4: MUSIC AND ARTS
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Describe artworks and performances
- Compare artistic forms and preferences
- Express informed opinions
- Plan a class arts event

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| artist | nghệ sĩ | The artist uses recycled wood. |
| portrait | tranh chân dung | This portrait shows her grandmother. |
| landscape | tranh phong cảnh | The landscape has cool colours. |
| sculpture | tác phẩm điêu khắc | The sculpture is made of stone. |
| exhibition | triển lãm | Our exhibition opens Friday. |
| performance | buổi biểu diễn | The performance lasted an hour. |
| audience | khán giả | The audience clapped loudly. |
| musician | nhạc sĩ/người chơi nhạc | The musician plays the flute. |
| instrument | nhạc cụ | A piano is a large instrument. |
| melody | giai điệu | The melody is gentle. |
| rhythm | nhịp điệu | Clap the rhythm. |
| traditional | truyền thống | We heard traditional music. |
| modern | hiện đại | The gallery displays modern art. |
| compose | sáng tác | She composed a short song. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `as + adjective + as / not as ... as` | So sánh bằng hoặc không bằng. | The flute is as expressive as the violin. / This hall is not as large as that one. |
| `be different from` | Nêu sự khác biệt. | Watercolour is different from oil painting. |
| `be like + noun/pronoun` | Nêu sự tương đồng hoặc đặc điểm giống. | The melody is like a gentle river. / Her voice is like her mother's. |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /ʃ/ and /ʒ/; examples: ['show, musician', 'vision, usual']; tips_vi: ['/ʃ/ không rung thanh; /ʒ/ có rung thanh.', 'Giữ môi hơi tròn và luồng hơi liên tục.']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 4: Music and Arts** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Nhi: Which picture do you prefer?  
Tom: The river landscape.  
Nhi: Why?  
Tom: Its colours are as calm as the evening sky.  
Nhi: I prefer the portrait.  
Tom: Is it like the artist's other work?  
Nhi: No, it is different from her bright paintings.  
Tom: The expression is powerful.  
Nhi: Yes. It makes me curious about the person.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Attention, performers. Saturday's arts festival will now begin at three o'clock because morning rain is expected. The photography exhibition remains in the library and opens at two thirty. The choir performs first in the main hall. The dance group follows at three twenty, and the student band begins at four. The clay workshop is different from the stage events: visitors may enter any time between three and five, but only twenty people can work at once. Please collect a number at the art room. Photography is allowed at exhibitions, but ask performers before taking close pictures and never use flash during the dance. Instruments may be stored in Room 5. All performers should arrive forty minutes before their act for a sound and safety check.

**Tasks:**

1. Why did the time change?
2. What opens at 2:30?
3. When does the band begin?
4. How many join clay at once?
5. What photo rule applies to dance?

**Answer key:** Morning rain is expected.; The photography exhibition.; At 4:00.; Twenty.; Ask permission and do not use flash.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> The old wall beside Green Street School was grey and covered with marks. Instead of repainting it one colour, students invited local artist Ms Diep to guide a mural project. First, they interviewed residents about the neighbourhood. Older people remembered rice fields, while younger children spoke about buses, shops, and a cleaner future. The final design showed a river moving from past to present. Traditional patterns appeared beside modern buildings, and paper boats carried wishes written by families. The students learned that a mural is different from a small painting: artists must consider distance, weather, public space, and many viewers. Teams enlarged the design on a grid and used safe, water-based paint. The finished wall was not as detailed as a portrait, but its story was clear. At the opening, musicians performed beside it. Residents said the combined music and image made the event feel like a shared memory rather than an ordinary decoration.

**Comprehension:**

1. Why was Ms Diep invited?
2. Who provided ideas?
3. What did the river represent?
4. How is a mural different?
5. What enhanced the opening?

**Answers:** To guide a mural project.; Local residents of different ages.; Movement from past to present.; It must suit distance, weather, public space, and many viewers.; Live music beside the mural.

**Writing:**

1. Write a comparison of two songs or artworks.
2. Write a 100-word exhibition review with evidence.
3. Create a programme note for an original class performance.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Sound-and-image association
- Gallery comparison walk
- Rhythm echo circle
- Artwork review conference
- Plan an inclusive arts festival

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: as beautiful than; correction: as beautiful as
- issue: different with; correction: different from
- issue: like always means enjoy; correction: It can also express similarity: The tune is like a wave.
- issue: Art opinions need no reasons.; correction: Support preferences with observable details.
