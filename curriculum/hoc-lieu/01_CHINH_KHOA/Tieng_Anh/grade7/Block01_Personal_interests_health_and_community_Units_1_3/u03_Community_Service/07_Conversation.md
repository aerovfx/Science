# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 3: Community Service

### Dialogue 1

Vy: What did your team do yesterday?  
Long: We cleaned the path near the lake.  
Vy: How many people joined?  
Long: Twelve students and three parents.  
Vy: Did you wear gloves?  
Long: Yes, and adults handled sharp objects.  
Vy: What did you learn?  
Long: Prevention is better than one clean-up.  
Vy: What will you do next?  
Long: We will make anti-litter signs.

### Dialogue 2

An: Our book drive ended on Friday.  
Mia: How many books did you collect?  
An: We received one hundred and six.  
Mia: Did you accept every book?  
An: No. We checked that books were clean and suitable.  
Mia: Where did they go?  
An: To two village reading corners.  
Mia: How did you know what they needed?  
An: We asked the librarians first.  
Mia: That made the project useful.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
- Listen again and choose one pronunciation point to improve.
