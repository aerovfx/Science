# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 3: COMMUNITY SERVICE
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 7 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Report completed community actions
- Discuss local needs respectfully
- Invite others to volunteer
- Reflect on impact and teamwork

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| community | cộng đồng | Our community shares a library. |
| volunteer | tình nguyện viên; tình nguyện | We volunteered at the food bank. |
| donate | quyên góp | Families donated clean books. |
| elderly | cao tuổi | We visited elderly neighbours. |
| homeless | vô gia cư | The group supports homeless people. |
| charity | tổ chức/hoạt động từ thiện | Check a charity before donating. |
| plant | trồng | Students planted trees. |
| clean-up | buổi dọn vệ sinh | The river clean-up began early. |
| litter | rác xả bừa bãi | We collected litter safely. |
| shelter | nơi tạm trú | The shelter needs blankets. |
| raise | gây quỹ | The concert raised money. |
| support | hỗ trợ | Neighbours supported the project. |
| impact | tác động | We measured our impact. |
| organise | tổ chức | Lan organised the book drive. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `S + V-ed / irregular past form` | Quá khứ đơn kể hành động đã kết thúc. | We cleaned the park yesterday. / They gave books last week. |
| `S + did not + V; Did + S + V?` | Phủ định và nghi vấn quá khứ đơn. | We did not waste water. / Did you join the drive? |
| `Time markers: yesterday, last..., ... ago` | Xác định thời điểm đã qua. | She volunteered two days ago. / We met last Saturday. |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Past -ed endings /t/, /d/, /ɪd/; examples: ['helped /t/', 'cleaned /d/', 'wanted /ɪd/']; tips_vi: ['Xét âm cuối của động từ, không xét chữ cái.', 'Sau /t/ hoặc /d/, -ed đọc /ɪd/.']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 3: Community Service** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Vy: What did your team do yesterday?  
Long: We cleaned the path near the lake.  
Vy: How many people joined?  
Long: Twelve students and three parents.  
Vy: Did you wear gloves?  
Long: Yes, and adults handled sharp objects.  
Vy: What did you learn?  
Long: Prevention is better than one clean-up.  
Vy: What will you do next?  
Long: We will make anti-litter signs.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Thank you for joining Saturday's garden project. Meet at the community centre at seven thirty, not at the garden. Team leaders will check names and give gloves and tools. At eight, we will walk together to the site. Group A will remove weeds; Group B will prepare soil; Group C will paint plant labels. Students must not use sharp tools without an adult. Bring a hat, water, and closed shoes. If the weather is dangerous, organisers will send a cancellation message by six thirty. Last year volunteers planted herbs, but several died because nobody planned watering. This time each class will care for one bed for a month. Before leaving, count the new plants and photograph the beds, not people, for the progress record.

**Tasks:**

1. Where do volunteers meet?
2. What does Group B do?
3. Which tools require an adult?
4. When is cancellation announced?
5. How will the project avoid last year's problem?

**Answer key:** At the community centre.; Prepare soil.; Sharp tools.; By 6:30.; Each class will water/care for a bed for a month.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Class 7A wanted to help a nearby neighbourhood. Their first idea was to collect toys, but their teacher asked them to investigate the real need. Four students interviewed the community librarian. She explained that children visited after school but had few science books and no quiet reading mats. The class changed its plan. They organised a book exchange, checked every donated book, and raised money for washable mats through a small music show. On delivery day, students labelled the books by topic and recorded the number given. They did not photograph children without permission. Two weeks later, the librarian reported that twenty-seven readers had used the new corner. The class felt proud, but they also noticed that some younger children needed help choosing books. Next month, volunteers will run a weekly reading hour. The project taught Class 7A that useful service begins with listening, includes safe and respectful action, and continues by checking whether the work actually helps.

**Comprehension:**

1. What was the first idea?
2. Who identified the real need?
3. How did students raise money?
4. What privacy rule did they follow?
5. What did the class learn?

**Answers:** Collect toys.; The community librarian.; Through a small music show.; No photos of children without permission.; Listen first, act respectfully, and evaluate impact.

**Writing:**

1. Write an 80-word report using five past verbs.
2. Create a respectful volunteer invitation.
3. Write a 130-word impact reflection with evidence.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Local-needs map
- Past-tense timeline
- -ed sound relay
- Volunteer planning meeting
- Impact evidence gallery

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Did they collected?; correction: Did they collect?
- issue: All -ed endings sound the same.; correction: They may be /t/, /d/, or /ɪd/.
- issue: Any donation is automatically useful.; correction: Confirm needs, quality, and consent first.
- issue: Impact equals number of photos.; correction: Use meaningful evidence such as participation or improved access.
