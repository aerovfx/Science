# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 1: HOBBIES
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 7 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Describe personal hobbies and routines
- Ask and answer about free-time interests
- Explain reasons for liking an activity
- Plan a small hobby club

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hobby | sở thích | My hobby is making models. |
| collect | sưu tầm | Lan collects postcards. |
| coin | đồng xu | This old coin is from Japan. |
| model | mô hình | We build plane models. |
| gardening | việc làm vườn | Gardening helps me relax. |
| photography | nhiếp ảnh | Photography teaches careful observation. |
| board game | trò chơi bàn cờ | We play a board game on Sunday. |
| creative | sáng tạo | Painting is a creative hobby. |
| patient | kiên nhẫn | You need to be patient with puzzles. |
| popular | phổ biến | Cycling is popular in our town. |
| indoors | trong nhà | I read indoors when it rains. |
| outdoors | ngoài trời | They prefer exercising outdoors. |
| equipment | thiết bị | This hobby needs little equipment. |
| take up | bắt đầu một sở thích | I want to take up chess. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `S + V(s/es) / do not, does not + V` | Thì hiện tại đơn diễn tả thói quen và sự thật. | Minh paints every evening. / She does not collect stamps. |
| `like/love/enjoy + V-ing; hate/dislike + V-ing` | Diễn tả yêu thích hoặc không thích hoạt động. | I enjoy reading comics. / He dislikes waiting. |
| `Do/Does + S + V?` | Hỏi về thói quen, sở thích. | Do you play chess? / Does Mai garden on Sundays? |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /ə/ and /ɜː/; examples: ['collect, away', 'bird, learn']; tips_vi: ['Âm /ə/ nhẹ và ngắn ở âm tiết không nhấn.', 'Kéo dài /ɜː/ và giữ lưỡi ở giữa.']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 1: Hobbies** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Mai: What do you do after school?  
Nam: I usually build paper models.  
Mai: How often do you do that?  
Nam: Three times a week.  
Mai: Why do you like it?  
Nam: It is creative and relaxing.  
Mai: Does it cost much?  
Nam: No. I reuse old paper.  
Mai: Can you teach me?  
Nam: Of course. Let's start tomorrow.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Hello students. The library is opening three hobby groups this term. The reading circle meets on Tuesday at four o'clock. Members choose one short story each month and share their favourite character. The chess group meets on Thursday in Room 12. Beginners are welcome, and the school provides boards. The nature photography group meets every second Saturday morning. Students explore the school garden and learn to take clear, respectful photographs without disturbing animals. Bring a phone or camera if you have one, but sharing is possible. Each group costs nothing. Please write your name and class on the registration card at the library desk by Friday. You may join one group first and change after two weeks. Ask Ms Hoa if you need more information.

**Tasks:**

1. Name the three groups.
2. When does reading meet?
3. Where does chess meet?
4. What rule protects animals?
5. How do students register?

**Answer key:** Reading, chess, and nature photography.; Tuesday at 4:00.; Room 12.; Do not disturb animals.; Complete a card at the library desk by Friday.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Every Saturday, thirteen-year-old An turns empty cartons into miniature houses. She began this hobby when her science teacher asked the class to reuse household waste. At first, her walls were uneven and the doors did not open. An watched short craft lessons, measured more carefully, and kept practising. Now she creates streets with shops, trees, and tiny bicycles. She never buys expensive materials. Her neighbours save clean cartons, bottle caps, and coloured paper for her. An often works with her younger brother, who paints the roofs. The activity is creative, but it also teaches them patience and teamwork. Last month, An displayed her model town at the school fair. Visitors left notes suggesting a library and a park. She plans to add both places. An says her hobby is special because rubbish gets a second life, while she gets new ideas and happy hours with her family.

**Comprehension:**

1. When does An make models?
2. Why did she begin?
3. How did she improve?
4. Who helps paint roofs?
5. Why is the hobby special?

**Answers:** Every Saturday.; A science reuse task inspired her.; She watched lessons, measured carefully, and practised.; Her younger brother.; It reuses waste and brings creativity and family time.

**Writing:**

1. Write 70 words about your hobby routine.
2. Create a club invitation with time, place, and equipment.
3. Write 100 words comparing two hobbies and recommend one.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Class hobby survey
- Vocabulary sorting: indoor/outdoor
- Information-gap weekly routines
- Design a recycled model
- Two-minute club pitch

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: He like draw.; correction: He likes drawing.
- issue: Using present continuous for a routine.; correction: Use present simple: I paint every Friday.
- issue: Does she collects?; correction: Does she collect?
- issue: Hobby means only sport.; correction: A hobby may be creative, social, physical, or intellectual.
