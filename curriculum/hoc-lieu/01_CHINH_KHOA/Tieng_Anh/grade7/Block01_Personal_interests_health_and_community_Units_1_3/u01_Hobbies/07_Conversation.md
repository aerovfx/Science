# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 1: Hobbies

### Dialogue 1

Mai: What do you do after school?  
Nam: I usually build paper models.  
Mai: How often do you do that?  
Nam: Three times a week.  
Mai: Why do you like it?  
Nam: It is creative and relaxing.  
Mai: Does it cost much?  
Nam: No. I reuse old paper.  
Mai: Can you teach me?  
Nam: Of course. Let's start tomorrow.

### Dialogue 2

Linh: Our class needs a hobby club.  
Ben: How about a photography club?  
Linh: I like taking photos, but we need cameras.  
Ben: Phones are enough for beginners.  
Linh: When can we meet?  
Ben: Friday afternoon works for me.  
Linh: Where should we go?  
Ben: The school garden has interesting subjects.  
Linh: Great. I'll make a poster.  
Ben: And I'll ask our teacher.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
- Listen again and choose one pronunciation point to improve.
