# ÔN TẬP CÓ ĐÁP ÁN / REVIEW WITH ANSWER KEY
## Unit 1: Hobbies

## A. Vocabulary and pronunciation

Use word cards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then practise target sounds, stress and intonation in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. She ___ stamps every weekend. (collect)
2. I enjoy ___ photos. (take)
3. Make negative: Nam plays chess.
4. Make a question: Lan gardens on Sunday.
5. Choose: My friends like/likes cycling.
6. Define 'take up'.
7. Indoor or outdoor: gardening
8. Correct: He enjoy paint.
9. Complete: ___ you collect coins?
10. Give one reason to read.
11. Write frequency phrase.
12. Plural: hobby
13. Opposite of indoors
14. Order: often / I / draw
15. Name reusable craft material.

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.
