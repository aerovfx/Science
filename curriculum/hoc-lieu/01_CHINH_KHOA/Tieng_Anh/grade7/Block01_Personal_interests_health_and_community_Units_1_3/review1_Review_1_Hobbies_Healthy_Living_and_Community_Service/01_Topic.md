## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Review 1: Hobbies, Healthy Living and Community Service** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Lan: We need to review hobbies, healthy living and community service.  
Minh: Let's divide the task and check the success criteria.  
Lan: I will find examples, but I will credit every source.  
Minh: Good. Then we can rehearse and give each other feedback.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> This Saturday, Green Street Youth Club will hold a wellness day from eight thirty to eleven thirty. Volunteers may join the garden, walking or creative team. The garden team will plant herbs. The walking team will guide elderly neighbours along a shaded route. The creative team will make cards for hospital patients. Eat breakfast, bring a refillable bottle and wear comfortable shoes. Garden volunteers also need gloves. Students under fourteen must arrive with a parent or school supervisor. Do not share participant photographs without agreement. At eleven, teams will count results and suggest one improvement. Registration closes Thursday evening. Meet beside the blue noticeboard ten minutes early. If the weather becomes unsafe, leaders will move every activity into the community hall.

**Tasks:**

1. What is the event or learning purpose?
2. When or where does one key action happen?
3. Name one item, team or resource mentioned.
4. What safety or respect instruction is given?
5. What final product or follow-up is required?

**Answer key:** Listen for the opening purpose statement.; Use the exact time or place stated in the script.; Identify one accurate concrete detail.; Follow the stated safety, privacy or respect rule.; Complete the stated reflection, presentation or report.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Phong builds bird feeders as a hobby. One Saturday, he notices plastic waste in a field near his home, so he invites classmates to help. They ask local residents what the area needs before making a plan. Wearing gloves, the students separate recyclable items and let an adult handle sharp objects. They put five feeders in safe places but do not touch nests or wild birds. For four weeks, the team observes from a distance and records the birds it sees. The outdoor work keeps them active, and they remember water, sunscreen and rest breaks. Neighbours begin using the new recycling bins. Phong learns that a hobby can support healthy living and community service when people listen, plan and act safely. The team counts its results and writes a short report instead of claiming that it solved every local problem. Next month, members will ask residents whether the field has remained cleaner.

**Comprehension:**

1. What is the main purpose of the text?
2. Name one action taken by the learners.
3. What safety or respect rule appears?
4. What evidence shows careful planning?
5. What lesson can students apply?

**Answers:** To show responsible learning through an integrated task.; They plan, cooperate and record a result.; They protect safety, privacy, culture or the environment.; They use checks, sources, measurements or feedback.; Use English to make an informed and respectful decision.

**Writing:**

1. Write an 80–100 word response about hobbies, healthy living and community service.
2. Create a practical notice or plan with purpose, details and one safety note.
3. Revise a first draft using two grammar patterns and six review words.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Vocabulary retrieval relay.
- Grammar error-detection stations.
- Pronunciation record-and-compare task.
- Reading and listening evidence table.
- Integrated group product and gallery feedback.

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Memorising a translation is enough.; correction: Use the word in context, retrieve it later and check collocation.
- issue: One grammar form works in every context.; correction: Choose form by meaning, time and communicative purpose.
- issue: The first online result is reliable.; correction: Check author, date, evidence and an appropriate trusted source.
- issue: A fluent presentation must be error-free.; correction: Clarity, repair strategies and evidence matter more than perfection.
