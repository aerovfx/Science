# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 2: Healthy Living

### Dialogue 1

Doctor: How do you feel today?  
Tuan: I have a headache and feel tired.  
Doctor: What time did you sleep?  
Tuan: After midnight.  
Doctor: You should rest and drink water.  
Tuan: Can I play football this afternoon?  
Doctor: Not today. Give your body time.  
Tuan: Should I take medicine?  
Doctor: Ask an adult and follow medical advice.  
Tuan: Thank you, doctor.

### Dialogue 2

Hoa: Let's improve our lunch boxes.  
Kim: Mine has noodles and fried chicken.  
Hoa: Add some vegetables or fruit.  
Kim: Good idea. What is in yours?  
Hoa: Rice, beans, carrots, and an orange.  
Kim: Is juice a healthy drink?  
Hoa: Sometimes, but water is best every day.  
Kim: I'll bring a water bottle.  
Hoa: Great. Small changes matter.


## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.

## Conversation challenge

- Add one greeting, one follow-up question and one closing.
- Record a 30–45 second pair dialogue only with appropriate consent.
- Listen again and choose one pronunciation point to improve.
