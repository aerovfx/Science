## Unit 2: Healthy Living

## A. Vocabulary and pronunciation

Use word cards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then practise target sounds, stress and intonation in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. Join with so: I was thirsty. I drank water.
2. Join with but: Tea is warm. It is not medicine.
3. Give advice for sunburn prevention.
4. Choose /f/ word: fit/vit
5. Complete: You ___ sleep earlier.
6. Name a protein food.
7. Make imperative: You should drink water.
8. Which connector shows choice?
9. Correct: You should to rest.
10. Define balanced diet.
11. Complete: I walked, ___ I felt better.
12. Give one hygiene habit.
13. Is 'Because tired.' a sentence?
14. Write a simple sentence about sleep.
15. Opposite idea connector?

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.
