# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 2: HEALTHY LIVING
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 7  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 7 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Describe healthy and unhealthy habits
- Give practical health advice
- Explain simple causes and results
- Create a balanced daily plan

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Independent clause: S + V (+ O).` | Câu đơn có một mệnh đề độc lập, diễn đạt một ý hoàn chỉnh. | Water keeps your body cool. / I walk to school. |
| `S + V, and/but/or/so + S + V` | Nối các câu đơn có quan hệ bổ sung, tương phản, lựa chọn hoặc kết quả. | I was tired, so I went to bed early. / Exercise is useful, but rest is important too. |
| `Imperative / should + base verb` | Đưa lời khuyên sức khỏe. | Drink more water. / You should rest. |

### Pronunciation

- focus: Sounds /f/ and /v/; examples: ['fit, laugh', 'vitamin, move']; tips_vi: ['Cắn nhẹ môi dưới; /f/ không rung thanh, /v/ có rung thanh.', 'Đặt tay lên cổ để cảm nhận độ rung.']

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 7 TIẾT / SEVEN-LESSON SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 2: Healthy Living** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Doctor: How do you feel today?  
Tuan: I have a headache and feel tired.  
Doctor: What time did you sleep?  
Tuan: After midnight.  
Doctor: You should rest and drink water.  
Tuan: Can I play football this afternoon?  
Doctor: Not today. Give your body time.  
Tuan: Should I take medicine?  
Doctor: Ask an adult and follow medical advice.  
Tuan: Thank you, doctor.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Next Monday our school begins a five-day wellness challenge. This is not a competition about weight or speed. It helps everyone notice healthy choices. On Monday, bring a reusable water bottle. Tuesday is colourful food day: include three natural colours in your meals. On Wednesday, take a twenty-minute walk with a friend or family member. Thursday is screen-break day. After every thirty minutes of homework on a device, look away, stretch, and rest your eyes. Friday focuses on sleep. Write one action that can make your bedtime calmer. Record each action honestly on your card. If illness, disability, or family circumstances make an activity difficult, choose a safe alternative with your teacher. At the end, write which habit you want to continue and why.

**Tasks:**

1. How long is the challenge?
2. What should students bring Monday?
3. What happens Wednesday?
4. What is Thursday's eye rule?
5. What should students do if an activity is difficult?

**Answer key:** Five days.; A reusable water bottle.; Take a twenty-minute walk.; Pause after thirty minutes, look away, stretch, rest eyes.; Choose a safe alternative with the teacher.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Minh often felt sleepy during the first lesson, so he decided to study his habits for one week. Each evening, he recorded bedtime, breakfast, exercise, water, and morning energy. On the first two nights, he played games until eleven thirty. He slept poorly and skipped breakfast. His energy score was only four out of ten. Then Minh changed three things. He put his phone outside the bedroom, prepared breakfast in the evening, and walked for twenty minutes after school. He also carried a bottle and drank water regularly. By Friday, he fell asleep earlier and could focus longer in class. His score rose to eight. Minh knows a one-week experiment cannot prove everything, but it showed him which routines helped. He now allows games after homework, stops screens an hour before bed, and asks his family to join his evening walk. Healthy living, he learned, is not one perfect choice; it is a set of small, repeatable decisions.

**Comprehension:**

1. Why did Minh start the experiment?
2. What did he record?
3. Name two changes.
4. How did his score change?
5. What lesson did he learn?

**Answers:** He felt sleepy in class.; Bedtime, breakfast, exercise, water, and energy.; Any two of phone outside, prepared breakfast, walking, water.; From four to eight.; Health comes from small repeatable choices.

**Writing:**

1. Write five linked simple sentences about your routine.
2. Write advice for a tired friend using should and imperatives.
3. Write a 120-word healthy-week plan.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Habit traffic-light sort
- Advice role-play
- Design a balanced lunch
- Combine sentence cards
- Seven-day wellbeing tracker

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 5–8 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Healthy means never eating treats.; correction: Health depends on balance, frequency, and context.
- issue: You should to drink.; correction: You should drink.
- issue: A fragment is a simple sentence.; correction: A sentence needs a complete subject–verb idea.
- issue: One habit cures every illness.; correction: Healthy habits support wellbeing; seek qualified help when needed.
