## Six-period unit rhythm

1. Lesson 1A: context, vocabulary, first pattern.
2. Lesson 1B: controlled listening and speaking.
3. Lesson 2A: second pattern, pair interaction.
4. Lesson 2B: phonics, listening discrimination, fluency.
5. Lesson 3A: reading and guided writing.
6. Lesson 3B: production, project, review and assessment.
