## Starter

## A. Vocabulary and phonics

Use flashcards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then read the phonics words aloud in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. Complete: ___! I'm Nam.
2. Complete: What's your ___?
3. Answer: How old are you?
4. Choose: I (am/is) happy.
5. Choose: It (am/is) red.
6. Write 8 in English.
7. Write the colour of grass.
8. Unscramble: koob.
9. Unscramble: lurer.
10. Complete: Open your ___, please.
11. Opposite of open.
12. Reply: How are you?
13. Complete: Nice to ___ you.
14. Make plural: one pencil, two ___.
15. Write a goodbye sentence.

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.
