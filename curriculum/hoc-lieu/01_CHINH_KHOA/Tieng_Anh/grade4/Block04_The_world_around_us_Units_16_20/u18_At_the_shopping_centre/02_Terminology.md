## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where is the gift shop? — It is next to the bakery.` | Hỏi và trả lời về shop locations and shopping. | Where is the gift shop? It is next to the bakery. / Where is the gift shop? It is next to the bakery. |
| `What do you want to buy? — I want to buy a card.` | Hỏi thêm thông tin và đáp lại bằng câu đầy đủ. | What do you want to buy? I want to buy a card. / What do you want to buy? I want to buy a card. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /s/; words: ['shop', 'bakery']
- sound: /c/; words: ['centre', 'bookshop']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
