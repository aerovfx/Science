# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 18: At the shopping centre

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

Where is the gift shop? It is next to the bakery. / Where is the gift shop? It is next to the bakery.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về shop locations and shopping.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời Where is the gift shop?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về shop locations and shopping, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 18: At the shopping centre**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
