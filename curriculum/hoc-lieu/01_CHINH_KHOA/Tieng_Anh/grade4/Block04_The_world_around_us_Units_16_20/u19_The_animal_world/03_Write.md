# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 19: The animal world

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

What are the lions doing? They are roaring loudly. / What are the lions doing? They are roaring loudly.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về animals and actions.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời What are the lions doing?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về animals and actions, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 19: The animal world**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
