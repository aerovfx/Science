## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What are the lions doing? — They are roaring loudly.` | Hỏi và trả lời về animals and actions. | What are the lions doing? They are roaring loudly. / What are the lions doing? They are roaring loudly. |
| `What animal do you like? — I like elephants.` | Hỏi thêm thông tin và đáp lại bằng câu đầy đủ. | What animal do you like? I like elephants. / What animal do you like? I like elephants. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /a/; words: ['animal', 'lion']
- sound: /w/; words: ['world', 'tiger']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
