# ÔN TẬP CÓ ĐÁP ÁN / REVIEW WITH ANSWER KEY
## Review 4 & Extension Activities

## A. Vocabulary and phonics

Use flashcards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then read the phonics words aloud in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. Complete: What's the ___ like?
2. Name weather with lots of rain.
3. Choose: It (is/are) windy.
4. Complete: I'm ___ a jacket.
5. Name footwear for your feet.
6. Complete: An elephant is ___ than a monkey.
7. Opposite comparative of bigger.
8. Ask for the museum's location.
9. Complete: Turn ___ at the corner.
10. Complete: Go ___ for two minutes.
11. Ask about a summer destination.
12. Complete: I'm going ___ the beach.
13. Choose: The giraffe is (tall/taller) than the monkey.
14. Name one place with wild animals.
15. Write one weather-and-clothes sentence.

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.
