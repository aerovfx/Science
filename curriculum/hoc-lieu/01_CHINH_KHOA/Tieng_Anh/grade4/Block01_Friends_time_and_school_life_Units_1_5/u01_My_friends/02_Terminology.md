## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where are you/he/she from? – I’m/He’s/She’s from ___.` | Hỏi và trả lời quê quán | Where are you from? – I’m from Viet Nam. / Where is Lucy from? – She’s from England. |
| `What is he/she like? – He/She is ___.` | Hỏi và mô tả tính cách | What is Nam like? – He is friendly. / What is Mary like? – She is kind. |
| `This is my friend, ___.` | Giới thiệu một người bạn | This is my friend, Minh. / This is my friend, Lily. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /f/; words: ['friend', 'fun', 'from']
- sound: /v/; words: ['very', 'Viet Nam', 'five']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
