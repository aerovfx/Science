# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 1: MY FRIENDS
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Chào hỏi và giới thiệu bạn bè
- Hỏi và trả lời quê quán
- Mô tả ngắn về ngoại hình và tính cách
- Viết hồ sơ đơn giản về một người bạn

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| friend | bạn | Lan is my friend. |
| new | mới | This is my new friend. |
| country | đất nước | What country is he from? |
| city | thành phố | Hue is a beautiful city. |
| Vietnamese | người/thuộc Việt Nam | Mai is Vietnamese. |
| friendly | thân thiện | Ben is very friendly. |
| kind | tốt bụng | My friend is kind. |
| tall | cao | Lucy is tall. |
| short | thấp; ngắn | Nam is short. |
| clever | thông minh | Minh is a clever pupil. |
| hobby | sở thích | Her hobby is drawing. |
| together | cùng nhau | We play together. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where are you/he/she from? – I’m/He’s/She’s from ___.` | Hỏi và trả lời quê quán | Where are you from? – I’m from Viet Nam. / Where is Lucy from? – She’s from England. |
| `What is he/she like? – He/She is ___.` | Hỏi và mô tả tính cách | What is Nam like? – He is friendly. / What is Mary like? – She is kind. |
| `This is my friend, ___.` | Giới thiệu một người bạn | This is my friend, Minh. / This is my friend, Lily. |

### Phonics

- sound: /f/; words: ['friend', 'fun', 'from']
- sound: /v/; words: ['very', 'Viet Nam', 'five']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 1: My friends** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Listen to Hoa talking about her two friends. Tom is from England. He is nine years old and very funny. He likes playing badminton. His favourite colour is blue. Aiko is from Japan. She is ten and very kind. She enjoys reading animal books and drawing cats. Hoa meets Tom at the sports club on Saturday. She talks to Aiko online every Sunday evening. All three children want to visit a zoo together one day.

**Tasks:**

1. Tom is from which country?
2. How old is Tom?
3. What sport does Tom like?
4. What books does Aiko enjoy?
5. When does Hoa talk to Aiko?

**Answer key:** England.; Nine.; Badminton.; Animal books.; Every Sunday evening.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> My name is Minh. I live in Da Nang. My new friend is Jack. He is from Australia, but he lives near my school now. Jack is tall and friendly. He has short brown hair. He likes football and making small paper planes. After school, we often play football in the park. On rainy days, we draw pictures at my house. Jack is learning Vietnamese, and I help him with new words. He helps me speak English. We are different, but we have lots of fun together.

**Comprehension:**

1. Where does Minh live?
2. Where is Jack from?
3. What does Jack look like?
4. What do they do after school?
5. How do the boys help each other?

**Answers:** Da Nang.; Australia.; He is tall and has short brown hair.; They often play football in the park.; Minh helps Jack with Vietnamese, and Jack helps Minh speak English.

**Writing:**

1. Hoàn thành hồ sơ bạn bè gồm tên, tuổi, quê quán và sở thích.
2. Viết 4–5 câu giới thiệu một người bạn.
3. Viết thiệp chào mừng một bạn mới đến lớp.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Friend bingo
- Country-card matching
- Guess my friend
- Pair interview
- Create a class friendship wall

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Dùng Where do you from?; correction: Dùng Where are you from?
- issue: Nhầm country và city; correction: Country là quốc gia; city là thành phố.
- issue: Dùng he cho bạn nữ; correction: Dùng she cho nữ, he cho nam.
- issue: Trả lời tính cách bằng tên nước; correction: What is ... like? cần tính từ như kind, friendly.
