# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 1: My friends

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

Where are you from? – I’m from Viet Nam. / Where is Lucy from? – She’s from England.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Hoàn thành hồ sơ bạn bè gồm tên, tuổi, quê quán và sở thích.
2. Viết 4–5 câu giới thiệu một người bạn.
3. Viết thiệp chào mừng một bạn mới đến lớp.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 1: My friends**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
