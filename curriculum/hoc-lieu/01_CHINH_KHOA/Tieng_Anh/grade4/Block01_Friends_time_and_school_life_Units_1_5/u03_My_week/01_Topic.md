# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 3: MY WEEK
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Gọi tên các ngày trong tuần
- Hỏi và nói hoạt động theo ngày
- Nói về tần suất đơn giản
- Lập kế hoạch tuần

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

| help | giúp đỡ | I help my mum. |
| usually | thường | I usually read at night. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What do you do on ___? – I ___.` | Hỏi hoạt động vào một ngày | What do you do on Monday? – I go to the art club. / What do you do on Sunday? – I visit my grandparents. |
| `When do you ___? – On ___.` | Hỏi ngày diễn ra hoạt động | When do you play chess? – On Friday. / When do you help Mum? – On Saturday. |
| `I usually ___ on ___.` | Nói thói quen theo tuần | I usually swim on Tuesday. / I usually read on Sunday. |

### Phonics

- sound: /w/; words: ['week', 'Wednesday', 'we']
- sound: /j/; words: ['yes', 'year', 'yellow']

- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 3: My week** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Here is Linda’s week. She has dance practice on Monday and Thursday. On Tuesday, she does homework with her sister. Wednesday afternoon is free, so Linda often draws in the park. Her family goes shopping on Friday evening. On Saturday morning, she takes her dog for a long walk. She visits her aunt in the afternoon. Sunday is family day. They cook lunch, play board games and prepare for Monday together.

**Tasks:**

1. Which days have dance practice?
2. Who studies with Linda?
3. Where does she draw?
4. When does the family shop?
5. What do they play on Sunday?

**Answer key:** Monday and Thursday.; Her sister.; In the park.; Friday evening.; Board games.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Phong plans his week carefully. On Monday, he plays basketball after school. Tuesday is for the science club, where he builds simple models. On Wednesday, he stays home and finishes his homework early. He practises the guitar on Thursday. Friday evening is family film time. On Saturday morning, Phong helps his mother at the market, then plays with his cousins. Sunday is quiet. He reads, prepares his school bag and checks the new week’s timetable. Phong likes having a plan because he remembers his work and still has time to relax.

**Comprehension:**

1. What does Phong do on Monday?
2. Which club does he attend?
3. When does he practise guitar?
4. Who does he play with on Saturday?
5. Why does he like a plan?

**Answers:** He plays basketball.; The science club.; On Thursday.; His cousins.; Because he remembers his work and has time to relax.

**Writing:**

1. Hoàn thành thời khóa biểu cá nhân 7 ngày.
2. Viết 5 câu về các hoạt động trong tuần.
3. Viết lời mời bạn tham gia một hoạt động cuối tuần.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Days chant
- Weekly-plan puzzle
- Find someone who
- Calendar information gap
- My balanced week gallery

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Không viết hoa tên ngày; correction: Tên ngày luôn viết hoa.
- issue: Dùng in Monday; correction: Dùng on Monday.
- issue: Nhầm Tuesday và Thursday; correction: Luyện âm đầu và nhận diện chữ viết.
- issue: Cho rằng week chỉ ngày đi học; correction: Một tuần gồm đủ bảy ngày.
