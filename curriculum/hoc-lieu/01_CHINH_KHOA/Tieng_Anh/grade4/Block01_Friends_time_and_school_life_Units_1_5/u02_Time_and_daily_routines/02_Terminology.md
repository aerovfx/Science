## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What time is it? – It’s ___.` | Hỏi và nói giờ | What time is it? – It’s eight o’clock. / What time is it? – It’s six thirty. |
| `What time do you ___? – I ___ at ___.` | Hỏi thời điểm của thói quen | What time do you get up? – I get up at six. / What time do you have dinner? – I have dinner at seven. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /t/; words: ['time', 'ten', 'tea']
- sound: /d/; words: ['dinner', 'do', 'day']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
