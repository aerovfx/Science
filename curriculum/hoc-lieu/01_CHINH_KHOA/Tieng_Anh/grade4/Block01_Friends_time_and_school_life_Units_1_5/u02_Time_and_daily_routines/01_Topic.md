## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Hỏi và nói giờ
- Nói về hoạt động hằng ngày
- Hỏi thời điểm diễn ra hoạt động
- Sắp xếp và trình bày lịch một ngày

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

|---|---|---|
| time | thời gian; giờ | What time is it? |
| o'clock | giờ đúng | It is seven o'clock. |
| get up | thức dậy | I get up at six. |

| dinner | bữa tối | My family has dinner together. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What time is it? – It’s ___.` | Hỏi và nói giờ | What time is it? – It’s eight o’clock. / What time is it? – It’s six thirty. |
| `What time do you ___? – I ___ at ___.` | Hỏi thời điểm của thói quen | What time do you get up? – I get up at six. / What time do you have dinner? – I have dinner at seven. |

### Phonics

- sound: /t/; words: ['time', 'ten', 'tea']
- sound: /d/; words: ['dinner', 'do', 'day']

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 2: Time and daily routines** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Nam has a busy Saturday. He gets up at seven o’clock and has breakfast at seven thirty. At eight, he helps his father water the plants. His swimming lesson starts at nine fifteen. Nam has lunch with his grandparents at twelve. In the afternoon, he reads comics and plays chess with his cousin. At six thirty, the family has dinner. Nam watches a short cartoon at eight and goes to bed at nine thirty.

**Tasks:**

1. What day is described?
2. When does Nam have breakfast?
3. What starts at nine fifteen?
4. Who plays chess with Nam?
5. When does he go to bed?

**Answer key:** Saturday.; At seven thirty.; His swimming lesson.; His cousin.; At nine thirty.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> On school days, Mai gets up at six o’clock. She washes her face and has breakfast at six thirty. She walks to school with her brother at seven. Classes begin at seven thirty. At eleven thirty, Mai has lunch at school. She goes home at four in the afternoon. First, she rests for twenty minutes. Then she does her homework. Her family has dinner at seven. In the evening, Mai reads a story or prepares her school bag. She goes to bed at nine thirty, so she is ready for a new day.

**Comprehension:**

1. When does Mai get up?
2. How does she go to school?
3. Where does she have lunch?
4. What does she do after resting?
5. Why does she go to bed at nine thirty?

**Answers:** At six o’clock.; She walks with her brother.; At school.; She does her homework.; So she is ready for a new day.

**Writing:**

1. Vẽ đồng hồ và viết 5 mốc giờ.
2. Viết 6 câu về một ngày đi học.
3. So sánh lịch ngày thường và cuối tuần bằng 5 câu.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Human clock
- Routine charades
- Timeline race
- Pair schedule interview
- Healthy-day poster

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Bỏ at trước giờ; correction: Dùng at + thời điểm: at seven.
- issue: Thêm s sau I; correction: I get, không phải I gets.
- issue: Nhầm morning và evening; correction: Morning trước trưa; evening sau chiều.
- issue: Đọc 6:30 là six thirteen; correction: 30 đọc thirty.
