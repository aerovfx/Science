# THẢO LUẬN / DISCUSSION
## Review 1 & Extension Activities

## Questions

1. Country-and-capital card match
2. Birthday-month class line
3. Weekly timetable information gap
4. Daily-routine mime and guess
5. Interview a new pen friend
6. Create a friend-profile poster

## Think — Pair — Share

1. Think and note key words for 60 seconds.
2. Rehearse one complete sentence with a partner.
3. Ask one follow-up question.
4. Share one similarity and one difference with the class.

## Communication rules

- English first; use gestures/pictures before asking for translation.
- Listen without interrupting and respond to the previous speaker.
- It is safe to pause, self-correct and try again.
- Do not imitate or laugh at a classmate's accent.
