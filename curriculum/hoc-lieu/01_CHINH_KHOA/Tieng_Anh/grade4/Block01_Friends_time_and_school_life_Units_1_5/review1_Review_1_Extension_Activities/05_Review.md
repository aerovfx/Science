# ÔN TẬP CÓ ĐÁP ÁN / REVIEW WITH ANSWER KEY
## Review 1 & Extension Activities

## A. Vocabulary and phonics

Use flashcards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then read the phonics words aloud in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. Complete: Where are you ___?
2. Answer: Where are you from?
3. Complete: I ___ in Da Nang.
4. Choose: What (day/time) is it today?
5. Day after Monday.
6. Complete: My birthday is ___ June.
7. Ask about a birthday.
8. Complete: I go to school ___ seven.
9. Put in order: you / where / live / do?
10. Choose: He (get/gets) up at six.
11. Choose: They (study/studies) English.
12. Name a time after midday.
13. Complete: Today ___ Friday.
14. Write 7:30 in words.
15. Write one sentence about your school.

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.

## E. Self-assessment

- [ ] I can understand the key words.
- [ ] I can ask and answer the unit question.
- [ ] I can read a short text.
- [ ] I can write a short connected response.
