# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 4: My birthday party

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

When is your birthday? – It’s in June. / When is your birthday? – It’s on June 12th.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết thiệp mời có ngày, giờ, nơi chốn.
2. Viết 5 câu mô tả một bữa tiệc.
3. Viết lời cảm ơn sau sinh nhật.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 4: My birthday party**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
