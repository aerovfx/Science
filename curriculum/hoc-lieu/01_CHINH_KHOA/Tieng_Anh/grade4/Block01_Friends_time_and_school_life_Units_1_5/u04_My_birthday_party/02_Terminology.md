## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `When is your birthday? – It’s in/on ___.` | Hỏi và nói tháng hoặc ngày sinh | When is your birthday? – It’s in June. / When is your birthday? – It’s on June 12th. |
| `Would you like to come to my party? – Yes, I’d love to./Sorry, I can’t.` | Mời và đáp lại lời mời | Would you like to come? – Yes, I’d love to. / Would you like to come? – Sorry, I can’t. |
| `There is/are ___ at the party.` | Mô tả đồ vật trong tiệc | There is a big cake. / There are ten balloons. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /p/; words: ['party', 'present', 'pink']
- sound: /b/; words: ['birthday', 'balloon', 'big']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
