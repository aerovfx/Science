# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 4: MY BIRTHDAY PARTY
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Hỏi và nói ngày sinh nhật
- Mời và đáp lại lời mời
- Nói về đồ ăn và hoạt động trong tiệc
- Viết thiệp mời sinh nhật

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

| party | bữa tiệc | The party is at my house. |

| game | trò chơi | We play a party game. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `When is your birthday? – It’s in/on ___.` | Hỏi và nói tháng hoặc ngày sinh | When is your birthday? – It’s in June. / When is your birthday? – It’s on June 12th. |
| `Would you like to come to my party? – Yes, I’d love to./Sorry, I can’t.` | Mời và đáp lại lời mời | Would you like to come? – Yes, I’d love to. / Would you like to come? – Sorry, I can’t. |
| `There is/are ___ at the party.` | Mô tả đồ vật trong tiệc | There is a big cake. / There are ten balloons. |

### Phonics

- sound: /p/; words: ['party', 'present', 'pink']
- sound: /b/; words: ['birthday', 'balloon', 'big']

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 4: My birthday party** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Sam is planning a birthday party for Sunday, September 15th. It will be in the community room near his home. The party begins at two thirty and ends at five. Sam wants a green dinosaur cake because dinosaurs are his favourite animals. The guests will make paper hats, play a treasure hunt and sing songs. Sam’s sister will prepare lemonade. Each child should bring a pencil for one game, but nobody needs to bring food.

**Tasks:**

1. What is the party date?
2. Where will it be?
3. When does it begin?
4. What cake does Sam want?
5. What should each child bring?

**Answer key:** Sunday, September 15th.; In the community room.; At two thirty.; A green dinosaur cake.; A pencil.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> It is Amy’s tenth birthday on Saturday. Her party starts at three o’clock in the garden. Amy makes colourful invitations for eight friends. Her father puts up balloons, and her mother makes a fruit cake. At the party, the children play musical statues and a guessing game. They drink orange juice and eat small sandwiches. Amy opens her presents after the games. Her favourite present is a book about space from her best friend, Kim. Before everyone goes home, they take a group photo. Amy feels happy and thanks her family and friends.

**Comprehension:**

1. How old is Amy?
2. Where is the party?
3. How many friends does she invite?
4. What games do they play?
5. What is Amy’s favourite present?

**Answers:** Ten.; In the garden.; Eight.; Musical statues and a guessing game.; A book about space.

**Writing:**

1. Viết thiệp mời có ngày, giờ, nơi chốn.
2. Viết 5 câu mô tả một bữa tiệc.
3. Viết lời cảm ơn sau sinh nhật.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Birthday-month line-up
- Invitation information gap
- Party-shop role-play
- Plan a class party
- Birthday card gallery

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Dùng on trước tháng riêng lẻ; correction: Dùng in May; on May 5th.
- issue: There is với danh từ số nhiều; correction: Dùng There are với số nhiều.
- issue: Trả lời lời mời chỉ bằng Yes; correction: Dùng Yes, I’d love to để lịch sự.
- issue: Nhầm birthday và birthday party; correction: Birthday là ngày sinh; party là bữa tiệc.
