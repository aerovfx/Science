## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Nhận biết và sử dụng từ vựng về daily routines and time.
- Hỏi và trả lời: When do you do homework?
- Trao đổi thêm bằng mẫu: What do you do in the evening?
- Đọc, nghe và viết đoạn ngắn A1 về daily routines and time.

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 14: Daily activities** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Mai: Hello, Nam. Let's practise.  
Nam: Good idea!  
Mai: When do you do homework?  
Nam: I do it in the afternoon.  
Nam: What do you do in the evening?  
Mai: I wash the dishes.  
Mai: Thank you. That was clear.  
Nam: You're welcome.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Listen to Ms Hoa's class report. Today the pupils are learning about daily routines and time. Mai asks, "When do you do homework?" Her partner answers, "I do it in the afternoon." Then Nam asks, "What do you do in the evening?" His partner says, "I wash the dishes." The class practises slowly, changes the people or places, and checks each answer with a partner. At the end, every group gives one new example. Ms Hoa reminds them to listen politely, speak clearly and use the new words in a complete sentence.

**Tasks:**

1. Circle the unit topic.
2. Write the first pupil's name.
3. Complete Mai's answer.
4. Tick the classroom action you hear.
5. Write one speaking rule.

**Answer key:** daily routines and time; Mai; I do it in the afternoon.; Groups give a new example.; Listen politely / speak clearly.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> On Sundays, Minh helps his family at home. In the morning, he gets up at seven and has breakfast. Then he waters the plants and cleans his desk. At noon, he has lunch with his grandparents. In the afternoon, he does his homework and plays badminton with his sister. After dinner, Minh washes the dishes while his father cleans the floor. At eight o'clock, the family watches a nature programme. Minh reads a short story before he goes to bed.

**Comprehension:**

1. Who or what is the text mainly about?
2. Where does the story happen?
3. Name one activity in the text.
4. What useful habit can we learn?
5. Is the main character positive? Why?

**Answers:** It is mainly about daily routines and time.; It happens in a place connected with daily routines and time.; Possible answer: a key activity named in the text.; We can plan carefully, help others and act safely.; Yes. The character learns, helps or behaves responsibly.

**Writing:**

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về daily routines and time.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời When do you do homework?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về daily routines and time, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Picture-word matching
- Listen and point
- Information-gap pair interview
- Sentence-card race
- Mini-poster and gallery walk

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES
