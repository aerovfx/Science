| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `When do you do homework? — I do it in the afternoon.` | Hỏi và trả lời về daily routines and time. | When do you do homework? I do it in the afternoon. / When do you do homework? I do it in the afternoon. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /d/; words: ['day', 'wake up']
- sound: /p/; words: ['play', 'have breakfast']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
