## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What does your mother do? — She is a nurse.` | Hỏi và trả lời về jobs and workplaces. | What does your mother do? She is a nurse. / What does your mother do? She is a nurse. |
| `Where does she work? — She works at a hospital.` | Hỏi thêm thông tin và đáp lại bằng câu đầy đủ. | Where does she work? She works at a hospital. / Where does she work? She works at a hospital. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /j/; words: ['job', 'teacher']
- sound: /w/; words: ['work', 'farmer']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
