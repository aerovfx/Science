# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 12: Jobs

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

What does your mother do? She is a nurse. / What does your mother do? She is a nurse.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về jobs and workplaces.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời What does your mother do?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về jobs and workplaces, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 12: Jobs**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
