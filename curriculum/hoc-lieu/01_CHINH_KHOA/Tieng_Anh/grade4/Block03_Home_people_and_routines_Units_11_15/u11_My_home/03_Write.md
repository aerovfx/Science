# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 11: My home

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

Where is your home? It is in Green Street. / Where is your home? It is in Green Street.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về rooms and home location.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời Where is your home?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về rooms and home location, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 11: My home**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
