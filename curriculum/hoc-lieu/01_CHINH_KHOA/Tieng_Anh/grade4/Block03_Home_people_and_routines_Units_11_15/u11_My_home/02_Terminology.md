## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where is your home? — It is in Green Street.` | Hỏi và trả lời về rooms and home location. | Where is your home? It is in Green Street. / Where is your home? It is in Green Street. |
| `Is there a garden? — Yes, there is.` | Hỏi thêm thông tin và đáp lại bằng câu đầy đủ. | Is there a garden? Yes, there is. / Is there a garden? Yes, there is. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /h/; words: ['home', 'house']
- sound: /r/; words: ['room', 'flat']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
