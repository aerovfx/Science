# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 13: Appearance

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

What does your brother look like? He is tall and strong. / What does your brother look like? He is tall and strong.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về people's appearance.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời What does your brother look like?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về people's appearance, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 13: Appearance**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
