## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What does your brother look like? — He is tall and strong.` | Hỏi và trả lời về people's appearance. | What does your brother look like? He is tall and strong. / What does your brother look like? He is tall and strong. |
| `What does he have? — He has short black hair.` | Hỏi thêm thông tin và đáp lại bằng câu đầy đủ. | What does he have? He has short black hair. / What does he have? He has short black hair. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /l/; words: ['look', 'tall']
- sound: /h/; words: ['hair', 'short']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
