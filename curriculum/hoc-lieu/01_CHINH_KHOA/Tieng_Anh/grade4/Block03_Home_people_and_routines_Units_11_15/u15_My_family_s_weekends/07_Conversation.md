# HỘI THOẠI / CONVERSATION PRACTICE
## Unit 15: My family's weekends

### Dialogue 1

Mai: Hello, Nam. Let's practise.  
Nam: Good idea!  
Mai: Where does your father go on Saturdays?  
Nam: He goes to the sports centre.  
Nam: What does he do there?  
Mai: He plays tennis.  
Mai: Thank you. That was clear.  
Nam: You're welcome.

### Dialogue 2

Ben: Can I ask you a question?  
Linh: Yes, of course.  
Ben: What does he do there?  
Linh: He plays tennis.  
Linh: What about you?  
Ben: I have a different answer.  
Linh: Please tell the class.

## Substitution practice

1. Underline information words that can change.
2. Replace one slot using a word card.
3. Change both speaker roles.
4. Close the book and keep the meaning, not exact wording.
