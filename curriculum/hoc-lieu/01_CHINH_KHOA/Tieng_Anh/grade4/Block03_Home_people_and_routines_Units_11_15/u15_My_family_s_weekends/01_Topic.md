## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Nhận biết và sử dụng từ vựng về family weekend plans.
- Hỏi và trả lời: Where does your father go on Saturdays?
- Trao đổi thêm bằng mẫu: What does he do there?
- Đọc, nghe và viết đoạn ngắn A1 về family weekend plans.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 15: My family's weekends** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

Mai: Hello, Nam. Let's practise.  
Nam: Good idea!  
Mai: Where does your father go on Saturdays?  
Nam: He goes to the sports centre.  
Nam: What does he do there?  
Mai: He plays tennis.  
Mai: Thank you. That was clear.  
Nam: You're welcome.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Listen to Ms Hoa's class report. Today the pupils are learning about family weekend plans. Mai asks, "Where does your father go on Saturdays?" Her partner answers, "He goes to the sports centre." Then Nam asks, "What does he do there?" His partner says, "He plays tennis." The class practises slowly, changes the people or places, and checks each answer with a partner. At the end, every group gives one new example. Ms Hoa reminds them to listen politely, speak clearly and use the new words in a complete sentence.

**Tasks:**

1. Circle the unit topic.
2. Write the first pupil's name.
3. Complete Mai's answer.
4. Tick the classroom action you hear.
5. Write one speaking rule.

**Answer key:** family weekend plans; Mai; He goes to the sports centre.; Groups give a new example.; Listen politely / speak clearly.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Hoa's family enjoys busy weekends. On Saturday morning, her father goes to the sports centre and plays tennis. Her mother buys food at the supermarket. Hoa and her brother visit the library to choose new books. In the afternoon, they help their grandparents in the garden. On Sunday, nobody goes to work or school. They cook lunch together and sometimes watch a funny film at home. Hoa likes Sunday evening best because everyone shares one good thing from the week.

**Comprehension:**

1. Who or what is the text mainly about?
2. Where does the story happen?
3. Name one activity in the text.
4. What useful habit can we learn?
5. Is the main character positive? Why?

**Answers:** It is mainly about family weekend plans.; It happens in a place connected with family weekend plans.; Possible answer: a key activity named in the text.; We can plan carefully, help others and act safely.; Yes. The character learns, helps or behaves responsibly.

**Writing:**

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về family weekend plans.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời Where does your father go on Saturdays?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về family weekend plans, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Picture-word matching
- Listen and point
- Information-gap pair interview
- Sentence-card race
- Mini-poster and gallery walk

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
