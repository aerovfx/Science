# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 15: My family's weekends

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

Where does your father go on Saturdays? He goes to the sports centre. / Where does your father go on Saturdays? He goes to the sports centre.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Viết 4 câu dùng 4 từ mới về family weekend plans.
2. Viết 5–6 câu trả lời Where does your father go on Saturdays?
3. Viết đoạn 50–60 từ về family weekend plans, có ít nhất hai mẫu câu của bài.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 15: My family's weekends**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
