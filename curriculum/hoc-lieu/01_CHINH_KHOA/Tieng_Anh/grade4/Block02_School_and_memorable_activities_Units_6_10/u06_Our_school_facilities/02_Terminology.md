## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where is the ___? – It’s ___.` | Hỏi và chỉ vị trí | Where is the library? – It’s next to the office. / Where is the music room? – It’s upstairs. |
| `Is there a ___? – Yes, there is./No, there isn’t.` | Hỏi trường có một nơi hay không | Is there a garden? – Yes, there is. / Is there a pool? – No, there isn’t. |
| `There are ___ in our school.` | Mô tả số lượng cơ sở vật chất | There are two computer rooms. / There are many trees in our school. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /l/; words: ['library', 'large', 'left']
- sound: /r/; words: ['room', 'right', 'read']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
