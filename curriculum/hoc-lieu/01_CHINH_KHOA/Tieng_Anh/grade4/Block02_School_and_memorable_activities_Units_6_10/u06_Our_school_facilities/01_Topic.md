# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 6: OUR SCHOOL FACILITIES
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Gọi tên cơ sở vật chất trường học
- Hỏi vị trí một địa điểm
- Mô tả trường bằng there is/are
- Đề xuất cách sử dụng và giữ gìn cơ sở vật chất

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| classroom | phòng học | Our classroom is upstairs. |
| library | thư viện | We read in the library. |
| computer room | phòng máy tính | The computer room is new. |
| music room | phòng âm nhạc | The drums are in the music room. |
| art room | phòng mỹ thuật | We paint in the art room. |
| playground | sân chơi | The playground is large. |
| garden | khu vườn | There are flowers in the garden. |
| building | tòa nhà | The library is in Building A. |
| upstairs | tầng trên | The lab is upstairs. |
| downstairs | tầng dưới | The office is downstairs. |
| next to | bên cạnh | The library is next to the office. |
| between | ở giữa | The garden is between two buildings. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where is the ___? – It’s ___.` | Hỏi và chỉ vị trí | Where is the library? – It’s next to the office. / Where is the music room? – It’s upstairs. |
| `Is there a ___? – Yes, there is./No, there isn’t.` | Hỏi trường có một nơi hay không | Is there a garden? – Yes, there is. / Is there a pool? – No, there isn’t. |
| `There are ___ in our school.` | Mô tả số lượng cơ sở vật chất | There are two computer rooms. / There are many trees in our school. |

### Phonics

- sound: /l/; words: ['library', 'large', 'left']
- sound: /r/; words: ['room', 'right', 'read']

- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 6: Our school facilities** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Welcome to Sunrise School. Please meet us at the main gate. Walk straight to the playground. The school office is on your left, next to the nurse’s room. The library is opposite the playground. Our new science room is upstairs in the yellow building. The toilets are downstairs near the stairs. Please do not run inside. If you need help, ask a teacher wearing a blue name card. We hope you enjoy your visit today.

**Tasks:**

1. Where should visitors meet?
2. What is next to the nurse’s room?
3. What is opposite the playground?
4. Where is the science room?
5. What should visitors not do inside?

**Answer key:** At the main gate.; The school office.; The library.; Upstairs in the yellow building.; Run.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Green Hill Primary School has three buildings and a wide playground. Building A has classrooms and the teachers’ office. The library is downstairs in Building B, between the art room and the computer room. It has colourful books and quiet reading corners. The music room is upstairs, so loud practice does not disturb readers. Behind Building C, there is a small garden. Pupils grow herbs and flowers there. Every class helps keep one area clean. Signs show visitors where to go, and ramps help everyone enter the buildings safely.

**Comprehension:**

1. How many buildings are there?
2. Where is the library?
3. Why is the music room upstairs?
4. What do pupils grow?
5. What helps everyone enter safely?

**Answers:** Three.; Downstairs in Building B, between the art room and computer room.; So practice does not disturb readers.; Herbs and flowers.; Ramps.

**Writing:**

1. Vẽ sơ đồ trường và ghi nhãn.
2. Viết 6 câu mô tả cơ sở vật chất.
3. Viết hướng dẫn đường từ cổng tới thư viện.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- School-map treasure hunt
- Facility label relay
- Information-gap map
- School guide role-play
- Dream school design

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: There is + danh từ số nhiều; correction: Dùng There are với số nhiều.
- issue: Nhầm next to và opposite; correction: next to là cạnh; opposite là đối diện.
- issue: Dùng Where the library is?; correction: Câu hỏi: Where is the library?
- issue: Chỉ mô tả đẹp mà quên an toàn; correction: Cơ sở vật chất cần sạch, an toàn, dễ tiếp cận.
