# NÓI VÀ THUYẾT TRÌNH / SPEAKING & PRESENTATION
## Unit 8: My favourite subjects

## Task

Prepare a 2–3 minute pair/group presentation for classmates.

## Suggested structure

1. Greeting and topic.
2. Show 4–6 picture/word cards.
3. Perform a target dialogue.
4. Share one personal example.
5. Ask the class one question.
6. Close and thank the audience.

## Useful language

- What’s your favourite subject? – My favourite subject is ___.
- Why do you like ___? – Because it’s ___.
- In ___, we ___.

## Activities

- Subject corners
- Because-chain game
- Mini class survey
- Subject activity charades
- Learning-strength badge

## Speaking rubric

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Message | Hard to understand | Some clear ideas | Clear and relevant | Clear with added detail |
| Language | Many missing words | Uses a frame | Accurate target pattern | Flexible use |
| Pronunciation | Often unclear | Understandable with repetition | Mostly clear | Clear rhythm and confidence |
| Interaction | No turn-taking | Needs prompts | Takes turns | Responds and follows up |
