# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 8: MY FAVOURITE SUBJECTS
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Nêu môn học yêu thích
- Hỏi và giải thích sở thích môn học
- Mô tả hoạt động trong môn học
- So sánh sở thích một cách tôn trọng

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## II. NGÔN NGỮ TRỌNG TÂM / LANGUAGE FOCUS

### Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Nghĩa tiếng Việt | Example |
|---|---|---|
| favourite | yêu thích | English is my favourite subject. |
| interesting | thú vị | Science is interesting. |
| useful | hữu ích | Maths is useful. |
| exciting | hào hứng | The experiment is exciting. |
| difficult | khó | This question is difficult. |
| easy | dễ | The song is easy. |
| experiment | thí nghiệm | We do an experiment. |
| map | bản đồ | We read a map. |
| number | con số | I like working with numbers. |
| story | câu chuyện | History has many stories. |
| because | bởi vì | I like Art because it is fun. |
| learn | học | We learn about plants. |

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What’s your favourite subject? – My favourite subject is ___.` | Hỏi và nói môn yêu thích | What’s your favourite subject? – My favourite subject is Science. / My favourite subject is Art. |
| `Why do you like ___? – Because it’s ___.` | Hỏi và giải thích lí do | Why do you like Maths? – Because it’s useful. / Why do you like Music? – Because it’s fun. |
| `In ___, we ___.` | Mô tả hoạt động trong môn học | In Science, we do experiments. / In Geography, we read maps. |

### Phonics

- sound: /iː/; words: ['easy', 'read', 'three']
- sound: /ɪ/; words: ['interesting', 'difficult', 'six']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 8: My favourite subjects** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Our class asked twenty pupils about favourite subjects. Six pupils chose Art because they enjoy colours and drawing. Five chose Science and said experiments were exciting. Four liked English best because they wanted to speak with new friends. Three chose Physical Education. They liked running and team games. Two pupils chose Maths because puzzles were fun. No subject was called the worst. Our teacher reminded us that a difficult subject can become easier with practice and help.

**Tasks:**

1. How many pupils joined the survey?
2. Which subject got six votes?
3. Why did five choose Science?
4. How many chose Maths?
5. What did the teacher remind pupils?

**Answer key:** Twenty.; Art.; Experiments were exciting.; Two.; Difficult subjects can become easier with practice and help.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Three friends enjoy different subjects. Ha loves Maths because she likes puzzles and numbers. She says a hard problem feels exciting when she finds the answer. Quan’s favourite subject is Physical Education. He enjoys moving, playing team games and learning to stay healthy. Lily likes English best. She reads short stories and practises speaking with friends. Sometimes she makes mistakes, but she keeps trying. The children help one another: Ha explains numbers, Quan leads active breaks, and Lily teaches new English words. Their different strengths make the study group better.

**Comprehension:**

1. Why does Ha like Maths?
2. What is Quan’s favourite subject?
3. What does Lily read?
4. What does Lily do after mistakes?
5. How do their differences help the group?

**Answers:** She likes puzzles and numbers.; Physical Education.; Short stories.; She keeps trying.; Each child contributes a different strength.

**Writing:**

1. Viết 5 câu về môn yêu thích.
2. Tạo biểu đồ khảo sát nhỏ và viết 3 nhận xét.
3. Viết lời khuyên cho bạn thấy một môn khó.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Subject corners
- Because-chain game
- Mini class survey
- Subject activity charades
- Learning-strength badge

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Dùng because rồi không có mệnh đề; correction: Sau because cần lí do: because it is fun.
- issue: Nhầm favourite là dễ nhất; correction: Môn yêu thích vẫn có thể khó.
- issue: Chê sở thích của bạn; correction: Sở thích khác nhau đều đáng tôn trọng.
- issue: Dùng What thay Why cho lí do; correction: Why hỏi lí do; trả lời bằng Because.
