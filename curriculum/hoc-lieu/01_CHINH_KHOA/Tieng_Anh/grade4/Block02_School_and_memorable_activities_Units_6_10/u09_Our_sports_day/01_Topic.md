## UNIT 9: OUR SPORTS DAY
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Gọi tên môn và sự kiện thể thao
- Hỏi và nói sẽ tham gia nội dung nào
- Cổ vũ và chúc mừng lịch sự
- Lập kế hoạch ngày hội thể thao an toàn

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 9: Our sports day** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Attention, pupils. Sports day will begin at eight o’clock tomorrow. The running races are on the field. Badminton and table tennis are in the hall. Please wear sports shoes, a hat and comfortable clothes. Bring a named water bottle. Eat breakfast before you come, but do not bring glass bottles. If you feel tired or hurt, stop and tell a teacher. Families may watch from the marked areas. At eleven thirty, everyone will meet for the closing ceremony.

**Tasks:**

1. When does sports day begin?
2. Where is table tennis?
3. Name two things pupils should wear.
4. What bottle should pupils bring?
5. What should a pupil do if hurt?

**Answer key:** At eight o’clock.; In the hall.; Any two: sports shoes, hat, comfortable clothes.; A named water bottle.; Stop and tell a teacher.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> River School holds sports day on Friday morning. The event begins with a safe warm-up for everyone. Younger pupils join short races, ball games and a beanbag throw. Class 4 enters football, badminton and a four-person relay. An drinks water before her race and waits behind the start line. Her team does not win, but they pass the baton carefully and finish together. They cheer for the winning team and receive a teamwork badge. Teachers say sports day is not only about medals. It is about health, fair play, courage and supporting friends.

**Comprehension:**

1. When is sports day?
2. What happens first?
3. Which events does Class 4 enter?
4. Does An’s team win?
5. What values does sports day teach?

**Answers:** Friday morning.; A safe warm-up.; Football, badminton and relay.; No.; Health, fair play, courage and supporting friends.

**Writing:**

1. Điền phiếu đăng kí nội dung thể thao.
2. Viết 5 quy tắc an toàn cho sports day.
3. Viết bản tin ngắn sau một cuộc thi.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Event mime
- Team registration role-play
- Fair-play scenario cards
- Sports commentary relay
- Plan a safe sports day

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Cho rằng sports day chỉ để thắng; correction: Mục tiêu còn là sức khỏe, hợp tác và fair play.
- issue: Thêm s sau will; correction: Sau will dùng động từ nguyên mẫu.
- issue: Quên khởi động và uống nước; correction: Đây là yêu cầu an toàn cơ bản.
- issue: Nhầm play badminton với do badminton; correction: Dùng play badminton/football/table tennis.
