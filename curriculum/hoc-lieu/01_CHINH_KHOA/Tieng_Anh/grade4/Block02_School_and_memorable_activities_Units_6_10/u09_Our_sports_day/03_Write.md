# ĐỌC VÀ VIẾT / READING & WRITING WORKSHOP
## Unit 9: Our sports day

## 1. Writing goal

Write 3–5 accurate, connected sentences using the unit vocabulary and patterns.

## 2. Model text

What event will you take part in? – I’ll take part in the relay. / I’ll take part in badminton.

Teacher and pupils notice: capital letter, word order, target pattern, punctuation, and one detail word.

## 3. Guided writing

1. Điền phiếu đăng kí nội dung thể thao.
2. Viết 5 quy tắc an toàn cho sports day.
3. Viết bản tin ngắn sau một cuộc thi.

### Planning frame

- Who/What: __________
- Key information: __________
- Detail 1: __________
- Detail 2: __________
- Closing sentence: __________

## 4. Independent writing

Write a mini-card/poster/message connected to **Unit 9: Our sports day**. Use at least four target words and one question–answer pair.

## 5. Peer check

- [ ] Meaning is clear.
- [ ] Target pattern is complete.
- [ ] Capitals, spelling, spacing and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Partner gives one praise and one helpful suggestion.
