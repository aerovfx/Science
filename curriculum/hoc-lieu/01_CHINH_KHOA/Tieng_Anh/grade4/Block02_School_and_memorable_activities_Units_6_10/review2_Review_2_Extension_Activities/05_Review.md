# ÔN TẬP CÓ ĐÁP ÁN / REVIEW WITH ANSWER KEY
## Review 2 & Extension Activities

## A. Vocabulary and phonics

Use flashcards to say, spell, classify and use the target words. Then read the phonics words aloud in random order.

## B. Exercises

1. Complete: My favourite ___ is Science.
2. Ask about today's lessons.
3. Choose: I (have/has) Music today.
4. Choose: She (like/likes) Art.
5. Answer positively: Can you swim?
6. Answer negatively: Can he dance?
7. Unscramble: ryalibr.
8. Name the place for outdoor play.
9. Complete: The lab is next ___ the library.
10. Complete: The library is ___ two rooms.
11. Ask for a location.
12. Name the subject with numbers.
13. Name the subject with songs.
14. Complete: I ___ football at break time.
15. Write one ability sentence.

## D. Four-skill check

- **Listen:** identify gist and two details from the teacher script.
- **Speak:** perform a four-turn exchange without reading every word.
- **Read:** locate evidence in the unit text.
- **Write:** produce 3–5 sentences and self-correct.
