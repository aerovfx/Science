# MỞ RỘNG VÀ DỰ ÁN NHỎ / EXTENSION & MINI-PROJECT
## Unit 10: Our summer holidays

## Optional challenges

1. Làm hộ chiếu hè giả định.
2. Thiết kế postcard thân thiện môi trường.
3. Theo dõi ba mục tiêu hè bằng lịch 30 ngày.

## Home–school connection

Teach a family member five words and one dialogue. Ask them to choose a picture card; respond in English and record the result in a learning log.

## Digital option

Create one slide or an audio poster. Use only copyright-safe images and do not publish personal information, faces or voices without consent.

## Product checklist

- [ ] At least six target words.
- [ ] One correct question–answer pattern.
- [ ] Clear voice or readable text.
- [ ] Original work and safe media use.
- [ ] One reflection: “Next time I will …”.
