## 2. Sentence pattern bank

| Pattern | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where will you go this summer? – I’ll go to ___.` | Hỏi và nói điểm đến | Where will you go this summer? – I’ll go to Da Lat. / I’ll go to the countryside. |
| `What will you do there? – I’ll ___.` | Hỏi và nói hoạt động dự định | What will you do there? – I’ll visit a waterfall. / I’ll play with my cousins. |
| `How will you get there? – By ___.` | Hỏi và nói phương tiện | How will you get there? – By train. / How will you get there? – By bus. |

## 3. Phonics and pronunciation

- sound: /h/; words: ['holiday', 'hot', 'home']
- sound: /tr/; words: ['travel', 'train', 'trip']

## 4. Retrieval routine

1. Cover the Vietnamese column and recall meaning.
2. Cover English and spell the word from a picture.
3. Use three words in a sentence-pattern exchange.
4. Record and listen: check final sounds, rhythm and intelligibility.
