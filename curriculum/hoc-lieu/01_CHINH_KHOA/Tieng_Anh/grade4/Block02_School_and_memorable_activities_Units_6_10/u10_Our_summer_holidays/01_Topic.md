# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 10: OUR SUMMER HOLIDAYS
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Nói về kế hoạch nghỉ hè
- Hỏi và nói nơi sẽ đến
- Nói hoạt động dự định
- Lập kế hoạch nghỉ hè an toàn và có ích

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `Where will you go this summer? – I’ll go to ___.` | Hỏi và nói điểm đến | Where will you go this summer? – I’ll go to Da Lat. / I’ll go to the countryside. |
| `What will you do there? – I’ll ___.` | Hỏi và nói hoạt động dự định | What will you do there? – I’ll visit a waterfall. / I’ll play with my cousins. |
| `How will you get there? – By ___.` | Hỏi và nói phương tiện | How will you get there? – By train. / How will you get there? – By bus. |

### Phonics

- sound: /h/; words: ['holiday', 'hot', 'home']
- sound: /tr/; words: ['travel', 'train', 'trip']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 10: Our summer holidays** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Khoa has three goals for the summer holiday. First, he will practise English for fifteen minutes each morning by reading and speaking aloud. Second, he will learn basic swimming with a teacher at the town pool every Tuesday and Thursday. Third, he will help his family sort paper and plastic for recycling. In August, Khoa’s family will take a short trip to Hue by train. He will visit a museum, try local food and send a postcard to his class.

**Tasks:**

1. How many summer goals does Khoa have?
2. How long will he practise English?
3. Where will he learn swimming?
4. What will his family recycle?
5. Where will the family travel in August?

**Answer key:** Three.; Fifteen minutes each morning.; At the town pool.; Paper and plastic.; Hue.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> This summer, Linh will spend one week in the countryside with her grandparents. She will travel there by bus with her mother. In the mornings, Linh will feed the chickens and water vegetables. Her grandfather will teach her to ride a bike safely on a quiet path. In the afternoons, she will read under a mango tree or play with her cousins. The family plans a picnic by the river, but they will check the weather first and stay away from deep water. Linh will keep a small diary and draw one picture each day.

**Comprehension:**

1. Who will Linh visit?
2. How will she travel?
3. What will she do in the mornings?
4. What safety steps will the family take?
5. How will Linh record the holiday?

**Answers:** Her grandparents.; By bus.; Feed chickens and water vegetables.; Check the weather and stay away from deep water.; In a diary with one picture each day.

**Writing:**

1. Viết kế hoạch hè gồm nơi, phương tiện và hoạt động.
2. Viết postcard 5–6 câu từ một điểm đến.
3. Lập ba mục tiêu hè cân bằng học tập, vận động và giúp đỡ.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Holiday destination cards
- Pack-my-bag challenge
- Travel-agent role-play
- Safe summer scenarios
- Summer plan presentation

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Thêm s sau will; correction: Sau will dùng động từ nguyên mẫu: will go.
- issue: Dùng by foot; correction: Dùng on foot; by bus/train/car.
- issue: Cho rằng nghỉ hè chỉ là đi xa; correction: Có thể nghỉ ngơi, học kỹ năng và giúp gia đình tại nhà.
- issue: Lập kế hoạch mà quên an toàn; correction: Cần người lớn, thời tiết, nước uống và quy tắc nơi đến.
