# KẾ HOẠCH BÀI DẠY / TEACHING PLAN
## UNIT 7: OUR TIMETABLES
**Môn học / Subject:** Tiếng Anh / English — Lớp 4  
**Bộ sách / Series:** Global Success — Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống  
**Thời lượng đề xuất / Suggested time:** 6 tiết

---

## I. YÊU CẦU CẦN ĐẠT / LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Gọi tên môn học
- Hỏi và nói môn học theo ngày
- Đọc thời khóa biểu
- Tạo và trình bày thời khóa biểu cân bằng

Học sinh sử dụng ngôn ngữ trong tình huống gần gũi, ưu tiên sự tự tin và khả năng hiểu được hơn việc nói hoàn hảo ngay từ đầu.

### Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Cách dùng | Model examples |
|---|---|---|
| `What subjects do you have on ___? – I have ___.` | Hỏi và nói các môn theo ngày | What subjects do you have on Monday? – I have Maths and English. / What subjects do you have on Friday? – I have Art and Music. |
| `When do you have ___? – I have it on ___.` | Hỏi ngày học một môn | When do you have Science? – I have it on Wednesday. / When do you have Art? – I have it on Friday. |
| `___ is before/after ___.` | Nói thứ tự môn học | Maths is before English. / Music is after break. |

### Phonics

- sound: /m/; words: ['Maths', 'Monday', 'Music']
- sound: /n/; words: ['nine', 'new', 'noon']

## III. ĐỒ DÙNG VÀ HỌC LIỆU / RESOURCES

- Flashcards, tranh tình huống, word cards, mini-whiteboards và phiếu học tập.
- Audio do giáo viên đọc từ listening script trong tài liệu này hoặc học liệu được cấp phép.
- Thiết bị ghi âm chỉ dùng khi có sự đồng ý của nhà trường/phụ huynh; không công khai giọng/hình học sinh.

## IV. TIẾN TRÌNH 6 BƯỚC / SIX-STAGE SEQUENCE

### 1. Warm-up and activation

- Greeting routine; quick game reviewing prerequisite language.
- Show one picture connected to **Unit 7: Our timetables** and elicit known words.
- State a child-friendly goal: “Today I can …”.

### 2. Vocabulary in context

- Present 4–6 words at a time through pictures/actions.
- Use the cycle: listen → point → repeat → choose → say.
- Check meaning with either/or questions, not translation alone.

### 3. Model dialogue and language pattern

**A:** Hello!  
**B:** Hi! Let’s practise English.

Teacher highlights the sentence pattern, models natural rhythm, and replaces one information slot at a time. Pupils practise in pairs with prompt cards.

### 4. Listening

**Teacher script (read twice):**

> Listen to Class 4B’s Monday timetable. School starts with Maths at eight o’clock. The second lesson is English. At nine thirty, pupils have a twenty-minute break. Music is after break, and lunch begins at eleven. In the afternoon, pupils study Vietnamese first. Their final lesson is Art, where they will make paper animals. The teacher reminds everyone to bring scissors and coloured paper, but to carry scissors safely in a closed pencil case.

**Tasks:**

1. What is the first subject?
2. When is break?
3. Which subject follows break?
4. What is the final lesson?
5. What materials should pupils bring?

**Answer key:** Maths.; At nine thirty.; Music.; Art.; Scissors and coloured paper.

First listening: gist. Second listening: details. Third optional listening: shadow one short sentence.

### 5. Reading and writing

**Reading text:**

> Class 4A has a busy but balanced Thursday. The first lesson is Vietnamese, followed by Maths. After a short break, pupils have Science. They observe leaves and write notes in groups. Lunch and quiet reading come next. In the afternoon, the class has English and Physical Education. Pupils bring trainers because they practise running outside. Their teacher puts difficult subjects earlier, when many children can focus well. Active lessons come later. Before going home, everyone checks Friday’s timetable and packs only the books they need.

**Comprehension:**

1. What is the first lesson?
2. What comes after Maths?
3. What do pupils observe?
4. Why do they bring trainers?
5. What do they do before going home?

**Answers:** Vietnamese.; A short break, then Science.; Leaves.; For running in Physical Education.; They check Friday’s timetable and pack needed books.

**Writing:**

1. Chép và hoàn thiện thời khóa biểu một ngày.
2. Viết 5 câu so sánh thứ tự các môn.
3. Viết tin nhắn nhắc bạn chuẩn bị cho ngày mai.

Use a model → shared writing → independent writing progression. Pupils underline the target pattern and check capitals, spacing and end punctuation.

### 6. Production, reflection and exit ticket

- Subject-picture match
- Timetable information gap
- Pack-the-bag race
- Design a balanced day
- Class timetable news

- Exit ticket: say one target sentence, identify one word, and write one short response.
- Self-assessment: 😊 I can do it; 😐 I need more practice; 🙋 I need help.

## V. PHÂN HÓA / DIFFERENTIATION

- **Support:** picture cues, sentence frames, rehearsal with a partner, fewer choices.
- **Core:** complete listening/reading tasks and produce 2–4 connected sentences.
- **Extension:** add reasons/details, ask a follow-up question, create a new dialogue.

## VI. ĐÁNH GIÁ / ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Achieving | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Needs repeated support | Gets some key words | Understands gist/details | Uses evidence to explain |
| Speaking | Single words with prompts | Short patterned response | Clear exchange | Adds detail/follow-up |
| Reading | Needs decoding help | Finds explicit detail | Understands short text | Makes a simple inference |
| Writing | Copies with errors | Completes a frame | Writes accurate sentences | Connects ideas creatively |
| Interaction | Reluctant | Participates with support | Takes turns politely | Supports partner actively |

## VII. LƯU Ý / TEACHING NOTES

- issue: Tên môn không viết hoa; correction: Tên môn trong tiếng Anh thường viết hoa trong tài liệu học này.
- issue: Dùng has sau I; correction: Dùng I have.
- issue: Nhầm timetable với clock; correction: Timetable là lịch môn học; clock cho biết giờ.
- issue: before và after đảo nghĩa; correction: Vẽ mũi tên thời gian để xác định trước/sau.
