### 🎭 Dialogue: "From Walking to Swinging"

**Anna**: Hey Ben, have you finished the physics homework about motion?

**Ben**: Uhh… kinda. I mean, walking to school is *uniform motion*, right? Constant speed, no drama.

**Anna**: Exactly! But when you run late and sprint—*that’s accelerated motion*.

**Ben**: Ha! And when I trip over my shoelace and fall—free fall motion! Gravity loves me too much.

**Anna**: (laughs) Poor you. But you know what’s cooler? Oscillations! Like when we play on the swing.

**Ben**: Oh yeah, that’s the motion that goes back and forth around one point, right?

**Anna**: Yup! It’s not just swings. Think of guitar strings, shock absorbers on bikes, even massage chairs.

**Ben**: Wait—so every time I sit on the swing, I’m basically… doing physics?

**Anna**: Exactly! Physics is everywhere. You just didn’t notice because you were too busy screaming, “Push me higher!”

**Ben**: (grins) Well, from now on, I’ll scream: “Push me higher… FOR SCIENCE!”

**Anna**: That’s the spirit! Newton would be proud.
