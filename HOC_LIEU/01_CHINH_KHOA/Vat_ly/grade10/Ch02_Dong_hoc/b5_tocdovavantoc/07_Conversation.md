Dialogue: Speed and Velocity

Teacher: Good morning, class! Today we’ll learn about speed and velocity. Can anyone tell me what speed is?

Student A: Speed is how fast something moves?

Teacher: Exactly! If a car travels 60 kilometers in one hour, its speed is 60 km/h.

Student B: And what about velocity? Is it the same?

Teacher: Not quite. Velocity is speed with direction. For example, if that car goes 60 km/h to the east, that’s velocity.

Student C: Oh, so if I run 5 m/s around the stadium, that’s speed. But what’s my velocity?

Teacher: Good question! If you come back to the same spot, your average velocity is zero.

Student A: Wow! So velocity depends on direction, not just speed.

Teacher: Correct. And in life, too: moving fast is not enough — you must know your direction.

Class: (laughs and nods)
